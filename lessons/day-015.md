今天这节换一组更贴近你实际工作的内容：**需求确认、会议里追问、跨团队交接、同事午饭聊天、hawker 点餐、Grab 上车、以及新加坡日常办事**。重点还是“说得出来”，不是学复杂语法。

# 40-minute Singapore English Speaking Lesson

## 1. Warm-up — 5 min

先大声说 3 遍：

> **Just to confirm, do you want me to change the frontend only?**  
> 确认一下，你是只希望我改前端吗？

> **I’ll take a look and get back to you.**  
> 我先看一下，然后回复你。

> **I don’t want to rush this and introduce more bugs.**  
> 我不想为了赶时间反而引入更多 bug。

> **Can we align on the expected behavior first?**  
> 我们可以先把预期行为对齐吗？

今天最重要的工作句型是：

> **Just to confirm...**

你不确定任何事情时，都可以用。

比如：

> Just to confirm, this is only for mobile, right?

> Just to confirm, we’re still targeting Friday, right?

> Just to confirm, the backend team will handle this part, right?

# 2. Office Communication & Meetings — 12 min

## 场景 A：需求不清楚

**PM:**  
We need to change the login flow.

**You:**  
Sure. Just to confirm, do you want to change the UI only, or the whole flow?

**PM:**  
The whole flow.

**You:**  
Got it. Do we already have the updated requirements?

**PM:**  
Not yet.

**You:**  
Okay. Can we align on the expected behavior before I start implementing it?

这里有 4 个高频句：

> **Just to confirm...**

> **Do we already have...?**

> **Can we align on...?**

> **before I start implementing it**

你可以替换：

> Can we align on the scope first?

> Can we align on the API contract first?

> Can we align on the deadline first?

---

## 场景 B：会议里追问细节

**Colleague:**  
We should probably cache this data.

**You:**  
What problem are we trying to solve with caching?

这个问题非常好用。

不要一上来就争论技术方案。

先问：

> **What problem are we trying to solve?**

然后：

> Is this mainly a performance issue?

> Are we seeing any problems in production?

> Do we have any data on this?

很像成熟工程师的表达方式。

完整练习：

> What problem are we trying to solve here?

> Is this mainly a performance issue?

> Do we have any data to support this?

---

## 场景 C：你觉得方案风险较高

**Engineer:**  
Let’s rewrite this part before the release.

**You:**  
I’m a little concerned about doing that right before the release.

**Engineer:**  
Why?

**You:**  
It touches quite a few areas, so the risk is relatively high. I’d prefer to make the smaller change first.

重点：

> **I’m a little concerned about...**

比直接说：

> This is dangerous.

自然很多。

再练：

> I’m a little concerned about the timeline.

> I’m a little concerned about the impact.

> I’m a little concerned about changing this so late.

非常实用的方案表达：

> **I’d prefer to...**

> I’d prefer to keep the current implementation for now.

> I’d prefer to ship the smaller version first.

---

## 场景 D：跨团队交接

**Backend Engineer:**  
The API is ready.

**You:**  
Great. Is the response format final?

**Backend Engineer:**  
Yes.

**You:**  
Perfect. I’ll integrate it today and let you know if I run into any issues.

这里记：

> **Is the response format final?**

> **I’ll integrate it today.**

> **If I run into any issues...**

**run into an issue**  
= 遇到问题

例如：

> I ran into an issue with the login flow.

> We ran into a performance problem.

比：

> I met a problem.

自然得多。

# 3. Colleague Small Talk & Socializing — 7 min

## 场景：午饭路上

**Colleague:**  
Where are you going for lunch?

**You:**  
I haven’t decided yet. Any recommendations?

**Colleague:**  
There’s a good chicken rice place nearby.

**You:**  
Nice. Is it usually crowded?

**Colleague:**  
A little.

**You:**  
Okay, let’s give it a try.

这里几个特别自然：

> **I haven’t decided yet.**

> **Any recommendations?**

> **Is it usually crowded?**

> **Let’s give it a try.**

---

## 同事问最近怎么样

**Colleague:**  
How’s everything going?

不要只回答：

> Good.

可以说：

> Pretty good. Work’s been a little busy this week, though.

或者：

> Not bad. I’m still getting settled in.

或者：

> Pretty good. I’m finally getting used to the commute.

这里：

> **Pretty good.**

比单纯：

> Very good.

更像日常英语。

---

## 和同事约饭

> **Are you free for lunch tomorrow?**

> **Do you want to grab coffee later?**

如果没空：

> I can’t today, but maybe tomorrow?

> I’ve got a meeting at one, so I need to head back soon.

**head back**  
= 回去

> I need to head back to the office.

> I’m going to head home.

# 4. Restaurant / Shopping / Ride-hailing — 7 min

## Hawker centre 点餐

今天练更真实的短句。

你：

> One chicken rice, please.

> Less rice, please.

> No skin.

> Chilli on the side, please.

在 hawker centre，这样说完全正常。

不用每一句都：

> Could I please...

如果你不知道哪个好吃：

> **What do you recommend?**

如果想问招牌：

> **What’s your most popular dish?**

---

## 听懂新加坡常见表达

店员可能问：

> **Having here or takeaway?**

意思是：

> 在这里吃还是打包？

你回答：

> Having here.

或者标准一点：

> For here, please.

另外在新加坡，你经常听到一个很简单的：

> **Can.**

比如：

你：

> Can I collect it tomorrow?

对方：

> Can.

意思就是：

> 可以。

你自己可以理解和偶尔使用，但工作场合建议还是说完整：

> Sure.

> That works.

> Yes, we can do that.

---

## Shopping

你想比较两个东西：

> **What’s the difference between these two?**

> **Which one would you recommend?**

如果只是看看：

> I’m just browsing, thanks.

如果决定不买：

> I’ll think about it.

---

## Grab / Taxi

司机：

> I’m at the pickup point.

你：

> I’m walking over now.

如果不知道具体位置：

> I’m near the main entrance.

> I’m next to the taxi stand.

如果司机走错：

> I think you may be at the other entrance.

比：

> You are wrong.

礼貌自然很多。

---

# 5. Practical Singapore Life Errands — 5 min

## 场景 A：Top up / EZ-Link

你可以问：

> **Where can I top up my card?**

**top up**  
= 充值

例如：

> I need to top up my EZ-Link card.

> Can I top it up with a credit card?

这是新加坡非常常用的词。

---

## 场景 B：公寓维修

> Hi, I’d like to report an issue with the air conditioner.

对方：

> What seems to be the problem?

你：

> It’s running, but it’s not cooling properly.

> Could someone come and take a look?

高频句：

> **What seems to be the problem?**

= 好像是什么问题？

> **Could someone take a look?**

= 可以让人来看一下吗？

---

## 场景 C：柜台办事

如果不知道需要什么资料：

> **What documents do I need to bring?**

如果不知道多久：

> **How long does the process usually take?**

如果不知道下一步：

> **What happens next?**

这三句银行、政府机构、学校、诊所都能用。

---

# 6. Common Grammar Fixes — 2 min

今天只纠正 4 个高频问题。

❌ `I have a question want to confirm.`  
✅ **I have a question I’d like to confirm.**

更自然直接：

✅ **Just to confirm...**

---

❌ `I met a problem.`  
✅ **I ran into a problem.**

英语里“遇到技术问题”通常：

> run into an issue/problem

---

❌ `Can you help me to check?`

不是错，但更自然：

✅ **Can you help me check?**

或者：

✅ **Could you take a look?**

---

❌ `I am agree.`  
✅ **I agree.**

因为 **agree 是动词**。

> I agree.

> I don’t agree.

> I completely agree.

不能：

> I am agree.

---

# 7. Final Speaking Drill + Review — 2 min

最后连续说这段 3 遍：

> We’re planning to change the login flow, but the requirements aren’t final yet. I’d like to confirm the expected behavior before I start implementing it. I’m also a little concerned about the timeline, because the change touches several areas. I’d prefer to make the smaller change first and reduce the risk.

第二遍，看关键词：

**login flow → requirements → confirm → timeline → risk → smaller change**

第三遍，不看原文，自己说。

## 今日复习卡

| 场景 | 直接说 |
|---|---|
| 确认需求 | **Just to confirm...** |
| 对齐预期 | **Can we align on the expected behavior first?** |
| 问问题本质 | **What problem are we trying to solve?** |
| 表达担心 | **I’m a little concerned about...** |
| 表达偏好 | **I’d prefer to...** |
| 遇到问题 | **I ran into an issue.** |
| 推荐一下 | **Any recommendations?** |
| 一起试试 | **Let’s give it a try.** |
| 回办公室 | **I need to head back.** |
| 充值 | **Where can I top up my card?** |
| 需要什么资料 | **What documents do I need to bring?** |
| 下一步 | **What happens next?** |

今天最值得练到脱口而出的 4 句：

> **Just to confirm...**  
> **What problem are we trying to solve?**  
> **I’m a little concerned about the timeline.**  
> **I’d prefer to make the smaller change first.**

这四句非常适合你在新加坡工程团队里开会、确认需求和讨论技术方案时使用。
