## Day 4｜40 分钟新加坡英语口语训练

今天的主题是：**“临时变化、插话、改会议时间、和同事社交，以及新加坡生活中的即时沟通。”**  
重点是把你在办公室和日常生活里会高频用到的句子练到能直接说出来。

---

## 0–10 分钟｜办公室：临时有变化，怎么说

先读两遍。

### 场景：开发计划发生变化

A: Are we still releasing this today?  
B: Probably not. We ran into an issue during testing.  
A: Is it a major problem?  
B: Not really, but we need a bit more time to fix it.  
A: Okay. What’s the new plan?  
B: We’re aiming for tomorrow morning instead.

这里最值得记的 5 个表达：

### 1. We ran into an issue.

意思：

**我们遇到了一个问题。**

比：

> We have a problem.

更自然，也没那么严重。

你可以说：

> We ran into an issue with the API.

> We ran into an issue during deployment.

> We ran into a performance issue.

---

### 2. We need a bit more time.

非常自然。

> We need a bit more time to test it.

> We need a bit more time to investigate.

> We need a bit more time to finish the migration.

注意：

❌ We need more some time.  
✅ We need **a bit more time**.

---

### 3. We’re aiming for…

表示：

**我们的目标时间是……**

> We’re aiming for Friday.

> We’re aiming for tomorrow afternoon.

> We’re aiming for a release next week.

这个句型特别适合工程师，因为它不像 “We will finish…” 那么绝对。

---

### 4. Instead

> We’re aiming for tomorrow morning instead.

= 改成明天上午。

例如：

> Let’s do it on Friday instead.

> Can we meet at 3 instead?

---

### 5. Are we still…?

这是今天的高频句型。

> Are we still meeting at 2?

> Are we still releasing today?

> Are we still going ahead with this plan?

### Speaking Drill

连续说：

> Are we still releasing today?

> We ran into an issue.

> We need a bit more time.

> We’re aiming for tomorrow instead.

然后把 release 换成：

> deploy  
> test  
> merge  
> launch

---

# 10–17 分钟｜会议里插话，不显得没礼貌

在多人会议里，你经常需要打断别人。

直接：

> Stop.

当然不行。

最常见的是：

### Sorry to interrupt, but…

> Sorry to interrupt, but can I ask a quick question?

> Sorry to interrupt, but I think we may be missing one thing.

---

### Can I jump in for a second?

非常自然。

> Can I jump in for a second?

= 我能插一句吗？

技术会议里很好用。

---

### Just one quick point.

如果你只想补充一句：

> Just one quick point — this might affect mobile users too.

---

### 对话

A: So the plan is to remove the old authentication flow—

B: Sorry to interrupt, but can I ask a quick question?

A: Sure.

B: Will this affect users who are already logged in?

A: Good question. We’ll need to check that.

---

### 常见错误

❌ Can I interrupt you?

语法不是错，但听起来有点生硬。

更自然：

✅ Sorry to interrupt, but…

或者：

✅ Can I jump in for a second?

---

## 练习

把这三个句子连续说 3 遍：

> Sorry to interrupt, but can I ask a quick question?

> Can I jump in for a second?

> Just one quick point.

目标不是背，而是以后在会议里能直接自动说出来。

---

# 17–23 分钟｜改会议时间

### 场景

A: Are you still free for our 3 pm meeting?  
B: Actually, something just came up. Could we push it back to 4?  
A: Yeah, 4 works for me.  
B: Great. Thanks for being flexible.

今天要记两个词：

### push it back

= 往后推。

> Can we push it back to 4?

> Can we push the meeting back by 30 minutes?

相反：

### move it up

= 提前。

> Can we move it up to 2?

---

### Something came up.

这是极其常用的一句。

意思：

**临时有点事情。**

例如：

> Sorry, something came up.

> Something just came up. Can we reschedule?

不用解释一大堆。

---

### Are you available at…?

> Are you available at 4 instead?

> Are you available tomorrow morning?

---

### 常见错误

❌ Can we change the meeting to later?

更自然：

✅ Can we **push the meeting back**?

❌ I have something suddenly.

✅ **Something came up.**

---

# 23–28 分钟｜同事闲聊：下班后约人

### 场景

A: Any plans after work?  
B: Not really. I might grab dinner somewhere nearby.  
A: A few of us are going for drinks. Want to join?  
B: Yeah, maybe. Where are you guys going?  
A: Probably somewhere around Tanjong Pagar.  
B: Sounds good. I might come along.

重点：

### Any plans after work?

非常自然。

类似：

> Any plans for the weekend?

> Any plans tonight?

---

### I might…

= 我可能……

> I might grab dinner nearby.

> I might stay home tonight.

> I might go to the gym.

比：

> Maybe I will…

更自然一些。

---

### A few of us are…

> A few of us are grabbing lunch.

> A few of us are getting coffee.

> A few of us are going out after work.

---

### I might come along.

= 我可能一起去。

**come along** 很口语。

---

## 30 秒输出

自己说：

> After work, I might ______.

> A few of my colleagues are ______.

> I might come along if ______.

# 28–33 分钟｜餐厅 / Hawker Centre：点餐听不懂怎么办

新加坡很多店员说话比较快，而且可能带口音。

最重要的是别慌。

### 场景

Staff: Here or takeaway?

You: Sorry?

Staff: Eating here or takeaway?

You: Oh, eating here.

Staff: Chilli?

You: Yes, but just a little, please.

---

### 高频句型

> Sorry, could you say that again?

> Sorry, a little slower, please.

> What was that?

第三个比较随意。

---

### 点餐

> Can I get one chicken rice?

> Less rice, please.

> Just a little chilli.

> No chilli, please.

> Takeaway, please.

---

### 新加坡你可能听到

> Can or not?

= 可以吗？

> Can.

= 可以。

> Cannot.

= 不可以。

这是典型 Singlish。

你**听得懂就行**，自己在工作和正式场合尽量用标准英语：

> Is that possible?

> Yes, that works.

> Sorry, that won’t work.

---

# 33–36 分钟｜购物：换货 / 尺寸不合适

### 场景

You: Hi, I bought this yesterday, but it’s a bit too small.  
Staff: Would you like to exchange it?  
You: Yes. Do you have one size up?  
Staff: Let me check.

重点：

### It’s a bit too…

> It’s a bit too small.

> It’s a bit too big.

> It’s a bit too expensive.

---

### one size up / down

> Do you have one size up?

= 有没有大一码？

> Do you have one size down?

= 有没有小一码？

---

### exchange

> Can I exchange this?

如果退款：

> Can I get a refund?

---

# 36–38 分钟｜生活办事：快递 / 公寓 / 网络

### 快递

> Could you leave it at the lobby?

> Could you call me when you arrive?

---

### 公寓网络坏了

> The Wi-Fi has been unstable since yesterday.

> It keeps disconnecting.

> Could someone take a look?

---

### 物业问什么时候可以上门

> What time will you be coming?

或者更自然：

> Roughly what time will someone come by?

# 38–40 分钟｜Grammar Fix + Compact Review

今天集中纠正 6 个错误。

❌ We met a problem.  
✅ **We ran into a problem.**

❌ We need more time for fix it.  
✅ **We need more time to fix it.**

❌ Can we delay the meeting to 4?  
✅ **Can we push the meeting back to 4?**

❌ Something suddenly happened to me.  
✅ **Something came up.**

❌ I maybe will go.  
✅ **I might go.**

❌ This is too small a little.  
✅ **This is a bit too small.**
