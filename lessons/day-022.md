# Day 22 | Disagreeing politely, proposing alternatives, and getting a decision

### Theme: 不直接硬顶，而是表达顾虑、给替代方案、推动对方做决定

今天重点练一个在新加坡职场非常实用的能力：**不同意时怎么说得自然、专业，而且还能继续推进事情**。

你会反复练这几个核心动作：

- 先承认对方观点：**I see your point.**
- 再表达顾虑：**My concern is...**
- 给替代方案：**Would it make sense to...?**
- 说清条件和时间：**If we keep the scope as it is, I should be able to finish by Friday.**
- 推动决策：**Which option would you prefer?**

同时继续复习：**I’m still waiting... / should be able to / by / on / before / future if**。

---

## 0–5 min | Warm-up: 先把“不同意但不冒犯”说顺

下面 5 句，每句大声说 3 遍。

> **I see your point, but I’m not sure that timeline is realistic.**  
> 我明白你的意思，但我不确定这个时间安排是否现实。

> **My concern is that we don’t have enough time for testing.**  
> 我担心的是，我们没有足够的测试时间。

> **Would it make sense to ship a smaller version first?**  
> 要不要考虑先上线一个更小的版本？

> **If we keep the scope as it is, I should be able to finish by Friday.**  
> 如果范围保持不变，我应该能在周五前完成。

> **I’m still waiting for the backend API, so I don’t want to overcommit.**  
> 我还在等后端 API，所以我不想承诺得太满。

快速复习时间介词：

- **by Friday** = 最迟周五完成
- **on Friday** = 周五当天发生
- **before Friday** = 周五之前，不包括周五

Future `if` 继续记住：

> **If we keep the scope small, we can release on Friday.**

不是：

> ❌ If we will keep the scope small...

---

# 5–17 min | Office Communication & Meetings

## 5–9 min | 场景 1：你不同意 deadline，但不想直接说 “No”

**Manager:**  
Can we finish everything by next Wednesday?

**You:**  
I see your point, but I’m not sure that’s realistic for next week.

**Manager:**  
What’s the main concern?

**You:**  
My concern is testing. I’m still waiting for the backend API, and we’ll need at least a day to test the integration properly.

**Manager:**  
So what would you suggest?

**You:**  
Would it make sense to ship the core flow first and move the reporting page to the next release?

这里最值得记住的不是一句话，而是一个三步结构：

> **I see your point.**  
> **My concern is...**  
> **Would it make sense to...?**

比直接说：

> That won’t work.

更适合会议场景。

### Reusable Pattern

> **I see your point, but my concern is + 名词 / that 从句.**

例如：

> I see your point, but my concern is the timeline.

> I see your point, but my concern is that we haven’t tested the edge cases yet.

> I see your point, but my concern is that the API may still change.

---

## 9–13 min | 场景 2：给两个方案，让对方选择

**PM:**  
We need both features in this release.

**You:**  
We can do that, but there’s a trade-off.

**PM:**  
What do you mean?

**You:**  
If we keep both features, I should be able to finish the implementation by Friday, but testing may need to continue into Monday.

**PM:**  
What’s the other option?

**You:**  
If we move the export feature to the next release, we should be able to finish and test the main flow before Friday.

**PM:**  
Okay. Let’s do that.

这里的核心不是“拒绝”，而是把选择说清楚：

> **Option A gives us ___, but ___.**

> **If we choose Option B, we should be able to ___.**

你也可以说：

> **There’s a trade-off.**  
> 这里需要做取舍。

> **The safer option would be to...**  
> 更稳妥的方案是……

> **The fastest option would be to...**  
> 最快的方案是……

### Deadline Drill

把下面三句各说 2 遍，体会区别：

> We should be able to finish **by Friday**.

> We’re planning to release **on Friday**.

> We need to finish testing **before Friday**.

---

## 13–15 min | 场景 3：讨论完了，但没人真正做决定

会议里最容易出现的情况是：大家聊了很多，但没人确认下一步。

你可以主动说：

> **So, which option would you prefer?**

或者：

> **Can we agree on the smaller scope for this release?**

确定后继续：

> **Great. I’ll take the frontend changes. Who’s taking the backend follow-up?**

如果还需要别人确认：

> **Should we wait for the product team to confirm before we proceed?**

非常实用的收尾句：

> **Just to confirm, we’re going with Option B, right?**

---

## 15–17 min | 2-minute Oral Substitution Drill

不要默读，直接开口。每个 Pattern 连续替换。

### Pattern 1

> **My concern is the timeline.**

替换：

- testing
- performance
- the API dependency
- the amount of scope

### Pattern 2

> **Would it make sense to ship a smaller version first?**

替换：

- test it with internal users first
- move this feature to the next release
- simplify the first version
- confirm the requirement before we start

### Pattern 3

> **If we keep the scope small, we should be able to finish by Friday.**

替换：

- get the API by noon / finish by tomorrow
- freeze the design today / start testing on Thursday
- remove this feature / release before Friday
- get approval today / proceed tomorrow

# 17–23 min | Colleague Small Talk & Socializing

## 17–20 min | 场景 1：朋友推荐东西，你不太认同

**Colleague:**  
You should try that new café downstairs. It’s the best one around here.

**You:**  
Really? I tried it once. It was okay, but I actually prefer the place across the street.

**Colleague:**  
Oh, why?

**You:**  
The coffee is a bit stronger, and it’s usually quieter.

这里不需要说：

> I disagree.

虽然语法没问题，但日常闲聊里更自然的是：

> **I actually prefer...**

> **I’m not really into...**

> **It was okay, but...**

比如：

> I’m not really into very sweet coffee.

> It was okay, but I probably wouldn’t queue for it again.

---

## 20–23 min | 场景 2：周末计划还没决定

**Colleague:**  
Are you going anywhere this weekend?

**You:**  
I haven’t decided yet. I might take my family somewhere, but we haven’t made up our minds.

**Colleague:**  
You could go to East Coast Park.

**You:**  
Yeah, that could work. If the weather is good, we might go in the afternoon.

几个很好用的自然表达：

> **I haven’t decided yet.**

> **We haven’t made up our minds.**  
> 我们还没决定。

> **That could work.**  
> 这个可以考虑。

Future `if`：

> **If the weather is good, we might go.**

不是：

> ❌ If the weather will be good...

---

# 23–30 min | Restaurant / Shopping / Ride-hailing

## 23–25 min | Restaurant：想点的菜没有了

**Staff:**  
Sorry, the grilled chicken is sold out.

**You:**  
No problem. What would you recommend instead?

**Staff:**  
The fish is quite popular.

**You:**  
Okay. If the chicken isn’t available, I’ll go with the fish.

可以直接记：

> **What would you recommend instead?**

> **I’ll go with the fish.**  
> 那我选鱼。

> **Would this work instead?**  
> 换成这个可以吗？

---

## 25–27 min | Shopping：没有你要的尺码

**You:**  
Do you have this in a larger size?

**Staff:**  
Not at this outlet, but another branch may have it.

**You:**  
Could you check which branch has it in stock?

**Staff:**  
Sure.

**You:**  
Thanks. If there’s one nearby, I can pick it up later today.

核心表达：

> **Do you have this in a larger size?**

> **Do you have it in stock?**

> **Could you check which branch has it?**

---

## 27–30 min | Grab：原来的上车点不方便

**Driver:**  
I can’t stop at the main entrance.

**You:**  
No problem. Would Pickup Point B work instead?

**Driver:**  
Yes, that’s fine.

**You:**  
Great. I’m walking over now. I should be there in about three minutes.

如果你还在等电梯：

> **I’m still waiting for the lift. I should be down in a few minutes.**

如果对方建议另一个地点：

> **That works for me.**

> **I’ll meet you there.**

注意：

> **in three minutes** = 从现在起三分钟后

> **after three minutes** 更常用于“经过三分钟后，再做某事”的叙述或指令。

---

# 30–35 min | Practical Singapore Life Errands

## 30–33 min | 场景 1：还没收到确认邮件

你预约了服务，但一直没收到确认。

**You:**  
Hi, I’m calling to check on my appointment. I’m still waiting for the confirmation email.

**Staff:**  
When is your appointment?

**You:**  
It’s on Monday morning.

**Staff:**  
You should receive the email today.

**You:**  
Okay. If I don’t receive it by this afternoon, should I call again?

这一段把今天的几个重点都串起来了：

> **I’m still waiting for...**

> **It’s on Monday.**

> **If I don’t receive it by this afternoon, should I...?**

---

## 33–35 min | 场景 2：确认材料最晚什么时候交

> **When do I need to submit these documents by?**

工作人员说：

> By Friday.

你可以确认：

> **So as long as I submit them before the end of Friday, that’s okay, right?**

如果你周五本人过去：

> **Can I submit them in person on Friday?**

对比：

> submit **by Friday** = 最迟周五交

> submit **on Friday** = 周五当天交

> submit **before Friday** = 周五之前交

# 35–38 min | Common Grammar Fixes

今天只纠正确实有问题的句子；本来就正确的表达不硬改。

### 1

❌ **I don’t agree this plan.**  
✅ **I don’t agree with this plan.**  
✅ **I disagree with this plan.**

`agree` 通常是 **agree with + 人 / 观点**。

---

### 2

❌ **If we will keep both features, we need more time.**  
✅ **If we keep both features, we’ll need more time.**

Future `if` 条件从句通常用一般现在时。

---

### 3

❌ **I can finish it until Friday.**  
如果意思是“最迟周五完成”：

✅ **I can finish it by Friday.**

但 `until Friday` 本身不是错误词组，它表示某个动作或状态“持续到周五”。例如：

> I’ll be working on this until Friday.

---

### 4

❌ **I’m waiting your reply.**  
✅ **I’m waiting for your reply.**

`wait for + 人 / 事物`。

---

### 5

❌ **Could you suggest me another option?**  
✅ **Could you suggest another option?**  
✅ **Could you suggest another option to me?**

`suggest` 不直接用 `suggest me something` 这个结构。

---

# 38–40 min | Final Speaking Challenge

## 第 1 分钟：Workplace Challenge

不看完整句，只看关键词，然后自己说一段：

**deadline → I see your point → concern → waiting for API → two options → by Friday → decision**

参考版本：

> I see your point, but I’m not sure the current timeline is realistic. My concern is that I’m still waiting for the backend API, and we also need enough time for testing. If we keep the scope as it is, I should be able to finish the implementation by Friday, but testing may continue into Monday. The safer option would be to move the export feature to the next release. Which option would you prefer?

## 第 2 分钟：Everyday Challenge

关键词：

**appointment → waiting → confirmation email → on Monday → if → by this afternoon → call again**

参考版本：

> Hi, I’m calling to check on my appointment. It’s on Monday morning, but I’m still waiting for the confirmation email. If I don’t receive it by this afternoon, should I call again, or can I still come in on Monday as planned?
