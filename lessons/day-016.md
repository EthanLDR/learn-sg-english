## Day 16｜40 分钟新加坡英语口语课

今天练一组之前没有系统覆盖过、但你在新加坡办公室会非常常用的内容：**确认范围、评估工作量、会议收尾、异步跟进、同事约饭、咖啡/饮料点单、Grab 定位，以及办事窗口沟通**。

重点仍然是：**短句、自然、能直接说出口。**

### 0–5 min｜Warm-up：先把嘴巴热起来

先连续读 3 遍：

> **Let me make sure I understand the scope correctly.**  
> 我确认一下我是不是正确理解了范围。

> **This may take a little longer than expected.**  
> 这个可能会比预期花更久一点。

> **I’ll send a quick update once I’ve checked it.**  
> 我确认完之后会发一个简短更新。

> **Before we wrap up, can we confirm the next steps?**  
> 在结束之前，我们可以确认一下下一步吗？

今天最核心的句型是：

> **Let me make sure...**

它比直接说 `I don't understand` 更自然。

例如：

> Let me make sure I understand this correctly.

> Let me make sure we’re talking about the same thing.

> Let me make sure I’ve got the timeline right.

## 5–17 min｜Office Communication & Meetings

### 场景 1：需求突然变大了

**PM:**  
Can we also add a filter and sorting to this page?

**You:**  
We can, but that changes the scope a bit.

**PM:**  
How much extra work would that be?

**You:**  
I’d need to check the implementation first, but I’d estimate another one or two days.

**PM:**  
Okay. Can you confirm later today?

**You:**  
Sure. I’ll take a look and send you an estimate.

这里重点学 4 句：

> **That changes the scope a bit.**  
> 这会让范围有一些变化。

> **How much extra work would that be?**  
> 这大概会多出多少工作量？

> **I’d estimate another one or two days.**  
> 我估计还需要一到两天。

> **I’ll send you an estimate.**  
> 我会给你一个工期评估。

替换练习：

> I’d estimate another few hours.

> I’d estimate about half a day.

> I’d estimate two to three days.

---

### 场景 2：你现在还不能承诺时间

经理问：

> Can you finish this by tomorrow?

不要习惯性回答：

> Yes, I can.

更专业：

> **I need to check the impact first before I commit to a timeline.**

简单版：

> **Let me check first before I commit.**

这里的：

> **commit to a timeline**

不是代码 commit。

意思是：

**正式承诺一个时间。**

再练：

> I don’t want to commit to Friday before I check the dependency.

> I should be able to give you a better estimate this afternoon.

非常适合工程师。

---

### 场景 3：会上确认谁做什么

**You:**  
Before we move on, can we confirm the ownership?

**Manager:**  
Sure.

**You:**  
I’ll handle the frontend changes, and the backend team will update the API. Is that correct?

**Manager:**  
Yes.

**You:**  
Great. I’ll start once the API contract is confirmed.

重点：

> **Can we confirm the ownership?**

这里的 `ownership`：

不是“所有权”。

工作里通常指：

**这件事谁负责。**

另外两个高频表达：

> **Who’s taking this?**  
> 这个谁负责？

> **I can take this one.**  
> 这个我可以负责。

---

### 场景 4：会议快结束，确认 action items

会议结束前可以说：

> **Before we wrap up, can we quickly go over the action items?**

`wrap up` = 收尾 / 结束。

例如：

> Let’s wrap up.

> We’re almost out of time, so let’s wrap up.

> Before we wrap up, I have one more question.

然后确认：

> So I’ll fix the frontend issue today.

> Jason will update the API.

> And we’ll test everything tomorrow.

> Does that sound right?

最后这一句：

> **Does that sound right?**

非常值得背。

它不是：

> 听起来对吗？

而是：

**我总结得对吗？**

## 17–24 min｜Colleague Small Talk & Socializing

### 场景 1：下楼吃午饭

**Colleague:**  
Are you heading out for lunch?

**You:**  
Yeah, in a few minutes. How about you?

**Colleague:**  
Same. Want to grab something together?

**You:**  
Sure. I’m up for anything.

这里新句型：

> **I’m up for anything.**

意思：

**吃什么我都可以。**

还可以说：

> I’m up for coffee.

> I’m up for trying somewhere new.

如果不想吃某种：

> I’m okay with anything except spicy food.

---

### 场景 2：同事问你最近忙不忙

**Colleague:**  
Busy week?

**You:**  
Yeah, a little. We’re trying to get a release out before Friday.

**Colleague:**  
Sounds stressful.

**You:**  
A bit, but it’s manageable.

这里：

> **It’s manageable.**

= 还应付得来。

比：

> It’s okay.

更自然。

你也可以说：

> It’s been pretty busy, but nothing too crazy.

`nothing too crazy` 是很自然的小聊天表达：

**还好，不算特别夸张。**

---

### 场景 3：别人问周末

**Colleague:**  
Doing anything this weekend?

你可以回答：

> I might take my family somewhere.

> I haven’t planned anything yet.

> I’m thinking of just taking it easy.

`take it easy`：

= 放松一下。

然后永远记得补：

> What about you?

这样 small talk 不会断。

---

## 24–31 min｜Restaurant / Shopping / Ride-hailing

### 饮料店 / 咖啡店

在新加坡点咖啡、奶茶很常用：

> **Can I get this with less sugar?**

> **No sugar, please.**

> **Less ice, please.**

> **Can I get it hot instead?**

如果店员说：

> Regular or large?

你：

> Regular is fine.

注意：

> **is fine**

是非常自然的：

> Regular is fine.

> This table is fine.

> 2 p.m. is fine.

---

### 付款

你可以问：

> **Can I pay by card?**

> **Do you take PayNow?**

或者：

> **Can we split the bill?**

如果你请客：

> **It’s on me.**

= 我来请。

如果同事说要 AA：

> Let’s split it.

---

### Shopping

你在商店试东西：

> This fits quite well.

> This feels a bit tight around the shoulders.

> Do you have the same one in another colour?

如果想想再买：

> I’ll have a think about it.

或者更通用：

> I’ll think about it.

---

### Grab / Taxi

司机：

> Where exactly are you?

你：

> **I’m by Lobby B, next to the pickup point.**

如果司机开过了：

> I think you just passed me.

如果你的位置难找：

> The entrance is on the other side of the building.

如果你马上出来：

> I’m on my way down.

和之前学过的：

> I’m coming down now.

都很自然。

## 31–36 min｜Practical Singapore Life Errands

### 场景 1：柜台办理事情

你：

> Hi, I’m here to update my details.

工作人员：

> Do you have the required documents?

你：

> I have my ID with me. Is there anything else I need?

很好用：

> **Is there anything else I need?**

可用于：

银行、诊所、学校、政府服务、物业。

---

### 场景 2：你不确定流程

说：

> **Could you explain what I need to do first?**

然后：

> What happens after that?

> Do I need to fill in a form?

> Can I do this online?

这三个问题在新加坡办事非常实用。

---

### 场景 3：预约 / 排队

你问：

> Do I need an appointment?

> Can I walk in?

`walk in`：

= 不预约直接去。

例如：

> Do you accept walk-ins?

如果要等：

> Roughly how long is the wait?

比：

> How long I need to wait?

正确自然很多。

---

## 36–38 min｜Common Grammar Fixes

今天纠正 5 个工作和生活里特别常见的错误。

❌ `We need to discuss about this.`  
✅ **We need to discuss this.**

因为：

> discuss + something

不用 `about`。

---

❌ `Please reply me.`  
✅ **Please reply to me.**

更自然工作表达：

> Please get back to me.

---

❌ `I arrived to the office.`  
✅ **I arrived at the office.**

> arrive at + 小地点

> arrive in + 城市/国家

例如：

> I arrived in Singapore yesterday.

---

❌ `Can you suggest me a restaurant?`  
✅ **Can you recommend a restaurant?**

或者：

> Can you suggest a restaurant?

不能：

> suggest me a restaurant

---

❌ `I will check it and tell you.`

不算错，但职场更自然：

✅ **I’ll check it and get back to you.**

---

## 38–40 min｜Final Speaking Drill

先完整读一遍：

> We’ve added a few new requirements, so the scope has changed slightly. I need to check the impact before I commit to a timeline. I should be able to send an estimate later today. Before we wrap up, I’d also like to confirm the ownership and next steps.

第二遍只看关键词：

**requirements → scope → impact → timeline → estimate → ownership → next steps**

第三遍自己说，不要逐句翻译。

# Day 16 Compact Review

今天复习时只看这 12 句：

| 场景 | 直接说 |
|---|---|
| 确认理解 | **Let me make sure I understand this correctly.** |
| 范围变化 | **That changes the scope a bit.** |
| 评估时间 | **I’d estimate another one or two days.** |
| 暂时不承诺 | **Let me check before I commit to a timeline.** |
| 确认负责人 | **Can we confirm the ownership?** |
| 我来负责 | **I can take this one.** |
| 会议收尾 | **Before we wrap up...** |
| 确认总结 | **Does that sound right?** |
| 什么都可以 | **I’m up for anything.** |
| 还能应付 | **It’s manageable.** |
| AA | **Can we split the bill?** |
| 等多久 | **Roughly how long is the wait?** |

今天最值得形成肌肉记忆的是：

> **Let me check before I commit to a timeline.**

> **Before we wrap up, can we confirm the next steps?**

> **Does that sound right?**

> **I’m up for anything.**

这几句会让你的英语从“语法基本正确”更接近**真实的新加坡办公室和日常交流英语**。
