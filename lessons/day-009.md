## Day 9｜40 分钟新加坡英语口语训练

今天的主题是：**“需求不清时怎么追问、怎么表达建议、怎么自然约同事，以及处理餐厅、购物和生活办事里的具体问题。”**  
重点依然是：**短句、自然、能马上用。**

### 0–10 分钟｜办公室：需求说得太模糊，怎么追问

先大声读两遍。

A: We need to improve the checkout experience.  
B: Sure. Is there a specific problem we’re trying to solve?  
A: Mainly the drop-off rate on mobile.  
B: Got it. Do we know where users are dropping off?  
A: Mostly on the payment step.  
B: Okay. It would help to look at the data before we decide on a solution.

这里有几个非常实用的句型。

**Is there a specific problem we’re trying to solve?**  
= 我们具体想解决什么问题？

比：

> What do you want?

自然很多。

你还可以说：

> What problem are we trying to solve here?

这是产品和工程会议里非常常见的一句。

**Do we know where…?**

> Do we know where users are dropping off?

> Do we know where the error is coming from?

> Do we know where the delay is happening?

**It would help to…**  
= 如果能……会有帮助。

> It would help to look at the data first.

> It would help to have a clear example.

> It would help to define the scope.

这句话非常适合你不想直接说：

> This requirement is unclear.

### Speaking Drill

连续说三轮：

> What problem are we trying to solve?

> Do we know where the issue is happening?

> It would help to look at the data first.

> It would help to define the scope.

然后替换关键词：

> performance  
> conversion  
> login  
> payment  
> mobile users

## 10–17 分钟｜会议：表达建议，不要每次都说 “I think”

场景：

A: We could add another confirmation step.  
B: That’s one option. Another approach would be to simplify the current flow.  
A: Why do you prefer that?  
B: It would reduce friction and be easier to maintain.  
A: That makes sense.

今天重点练这些表达：

**That’s one option.**

= 这是一种方案。

如果你不想直接否定别人，非常好用。

**Another approach would be to…**

> Another approach would be to cache the result.

> Another approach would be to move this logic to the backend.

**The benefit is that…**

> The benefit is that it’s simpler.

> The benefit is that it reduces network requests.

**The downside is that…**

> The downside is that it takes more time.

> The downside is that it adds complexity.

这四个句型可以直接组成一段很成熟的技术表达：

> That’s one option. Another approach would be to move this logic to the backend. The benefit is that it’s easier to maintain. The downside is that it may take longer to implement.

## 17–23 分钟｜和同事聊天：自然约下班吃饭

场景：

A: Are you doing anything after work?  
B: Not really. Why?  
A: A few of us are getting dinner nearby. Want to join us?  
B: Sure, I’m in. What time are you heading out?  
A: Probably around 6:30.  
B: Sounds good.

重点：

**Are you doing anything after work?**

= 下班后有安排吗？

**Want to join us?**

比：

> Do you want to come with us?

更口语。

**I’m in.**

= 我参加 / 算我一个。

很自然。

**What time are you heading out?**

= 你们几点出发？

**head out** = 出发 / 离开

> I’m heading out around six.

> What time are you heading out?

### 快速 Drill

> Want to join us?

> Yeah, I’m in.

> What time are you heading out?

> Around 6:30 works for me.

## 23–28 分钟｜闲聊：别人说周末安排，怎么继续聊

A: I’m thinking of going to Sentosa this weekend.  
B: Nice. What are you planning to do there?  
A: Probably just walk around and get some food.  
B: Sounds relaxing. Are you going with friends?  
A: Yeah, a couple of friends from work.

今天练这种**连续接话**：

> What are you planning to do there?

> Are you going with anyone?

> Have you been there before?

> How long are you planning to stay?

> Sounds relaxing.

不要只会：

> Oh, okay.

### 自由输出 1 分钟

假设同事说：

> I’m going to Johor Bahru this weekend.

你至少接 4 句：

> Oh nice.  
> What are you planning to do there?  
> Are you going with anyone?  
> Have you been there before?

## 28–32 分钟｜餐厅：饮食要求和改菜

场景：

You: Hi, does this come with peanuts?  
Staff: Yes, the sauce contains peanuts.  
You: Is it possible to have it without the sauce?  
Staff: Sure.  
You: Great, thanks.

重点句型：

**Does this come with…?**

> Does this come with rice?

> Does this come with a drink?

> Does this come with chilli sauce?

**Does this contain…?**

> Does this contain peanuts?

> Does this contain dairy?

**Is it possible to have it without…?**

> Is it possible to have it without onions?

> Is it possible to have the sauce on the side?

**on the side**  
= 另外放，不要直接浇上去。

> Could I have the sauce on the side?

非常实用。

## 32–35 分钟｜购物：想退货，但不确定政策

场景：

You: Hi, I bought this yesterday, but I changed my mind.  
Staff: Do you have the receipt?  
You: Yes. I just wanted to check if I can return it.  
Staff: As long as it hasn’t been used.  
You: Okay, that’s fine.

重点：

**I changed my mind.**

= 我改变主意了。

**I just wanted to check if…**

非常柔和、实用。

> I just wanted to check if returns are allowed.

> I just wanted to check if this is covered by warranty.

> I just wanted to check if I need an appointment.

比直接：

> Can I?

稍微更礼貌。

## 35–38 分钟｜新加坡生活办事：修改地址

场景：银行 / 电信 / 保险。

You: Hi, I recently moved, and I need to update my address.  
Staff: Sure. Do you have proof of address?  
You: Yes. Is a tenancy agreement acceptable?  
Staff: Yes, that works.  
You: Great. How long will the update take?

重点：

**I recently moved.**

= 我最近搬家了。

**I need to update my address.**

**Is ___ acceptable?**

> Is a digital copy acceptable?

> Is my tenancy agreement acceptable?

**How long will the update take?**

比：

> How many days I need?

自然很多。

## 38–40 分钟｜Grammar Fix + Compact Review

今天纠正 6 个常见错误：

❌ What problem we are trying to solve?  
✅ **What problem are we trying to solve?**

❌ Another way is move it to backend.  
✅ **Another approach would be to move it to the backend.**

❌ The benefit is it more simple.  
✅ **The benefit is that it’s simpler.**

❌ I join you guys.  
✅ **I’m in.**  
✅ **I’ll join you guys.**

❌ This has peanut?  
✅ **Does this contain peanuts?**

❌ I want update my address.  
✅ **I need to update my address.**

> What problem are we trying to solve?

> It would help to look at the data first.

> That’s one option.

> Yeah, I’m in.

> I just wanted to check if I can return it.

> I need to update my address.
