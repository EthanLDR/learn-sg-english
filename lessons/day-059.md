# Day 59 | Just a heads-up: share changes early and make the next action clear

### 今日主题：情况有变化时，及时通知相关的人，并说清楚对方需不需要行动

今天的新技能是 **give a heads-up → explain the impact → say whether action is needed → promise the next update**（提前提醒 → 说明影响 → 说清是否需要行动 → 约定下一次更新）。昨天练的是核对不一致的信息；今天练的是**发现变化后，如何用简短英语主动同步，而不是等别人追问**。

> **Just a heads-up: the test build may be delayed.** 提前说一声：测试包可能会延迟。
>
> **This could affect QA's afternoon testing.** 这可能影响 QA 下午的测试。
>
> **No action needed from you for now.** 你目前不用做什么。
>
> **Could you hold off on the upload tests until I confirm?** 能先暂停上传相关测试，等我确认吗？
>
> **I'll keep you posted.** 有进展我会继续告诉你。

**heads-up** = 提前提醒（比较口语）；**FYI** = 供你知悉；**action required** = 需要采取行动；**loop someone in** = 把某人加入沟通。关键区别：**FYI 不等于要求对方立刻处理**。技术更新只分享获准公开给该群组的信息，避免把私有日志、密钥或用户数据发到无关频道。

本课穿插复习 **I'm still waiting for / should be able to / by、on、before / 未来 if 条件句**。以下人物、工作项目和新加坡生活流程均为模拟练习，真实操作以公司规定或服务商官方说明为准。

**严格计时 40 分钟：0–5（5）+ 5–17（12）+ 17–23（6）+ 23–30（7）+ 30–35（5）+ 35–37（2）+ 37–38（1）+ 38–40（2）= 40。** 每段的朗读、替换、自由表达和自检都已计入时间。

## 0–5 min | Warm-up：一句提醒，带上影响和下一步

**0–2 min｜四句各读三遍，剩余时间连起来说：**

> **Just a heads-up: the plan has changed.** 提前说一声：计划变了。
>
> **This may affect our meeting time.** 这可能影响会议时间。
>
> **You don't need to do anything yet.** 你暂时不用处理。
>
> **I'll update you as soon as I know more.** 我有更多消息就告诉你。

**2–4 min｜每轮一分钟；先读对话，再遮住 You 的台词：**

**Colleague:** Is the demo still at 3 p.m.?

**You:** Just a heads-up: we're waiting for a new build, so the time may change.

**Colleague:** Should I update the invite now?

**You:** Not yet. I'll confirm the new time before you change it.

第二轮替换 **demo → UI review**、**new build → QA feedback**、**3 p.m. → 4 p.m.**。注意 **may change** 不是 **has changed**。

**4–5 min｜中文转英文，随后核对：**

“我还在等确认。” → **I'm still waiting for confirmation.**

“如果中午前收到，我应该能最迟下午两点通知大家。” → **If I get it by noon, I should be able to update everyone by 2 p.m.**

“现在你不需要做任何事。” → **No action needed from you right now.**

## 5–17 min | Office Communication & Meetings

### 5–9 min | Scene 1：API 依赖可能影响 QA 测试，要提前通知

**QA:** Are we still running the upload tests at 2 p.m.?

**You:** Just a heads-up: the API response hasn't been finalized yet.

**QA:** Does that affect our test session?

**You:** It may affect the upload cases. I'm still waiting for the updated API contract.

**QA:** Should I cancel the whole session?

**You:** Not yet. Could you hold off on the upload cases and continue the unrelated checks?

**QA:** Sure. When will you know more?

**You:** If the contract arrives by noon, I should be able to give you an update by 1 p.m. I'll let you know if that changes.

**5–7 min：**分角色朗读两轮，第二轮遮住 You。**7–8 min：**脱稿回答：What is unconfirmed? Which cases are affected? What should QA do now? **8–9 min：**用 40 秒说完整提醒，剩余 20 秒再说一次行动要求：

> **Just a heads-up: ___. This may affect ___. I'm still waiting for ___. For now, could you ___? If ___, I should be able to update you by ___.**

**hold off on + 名词 / -ing** = 暂缓某事。**Hold off on the upload tests** 不等于取消整个 QA 测试；不要把“可能影响”说成“确定失败”。

### 9–13 min | Scene 2：会议中决定应该通知哪些人

**Lead:** The button label has changed. Do we need to tell everyone?

**You:** Let's identify who's affected first. The designer and QA need the new wording.

**PM:** What about the backend team?

**You:** I don't think they need to take action for a text-only change, unless the API contract also changes.

**Lead:** How would you send the update?

**You:** I'd post a short summary in the project channel: what changed, which screens are affected, and what QA needs to verify.

**PM:** Good. Can you tag the right people?

**You:** Yes. I'll loop in the designer and QA, and make the required action explicit.

**9–11 min：**读两轮；第二轮把 **button label → empty-state message**。**11–12 min：**按 **change → affected teams → FYI or action → next check** 复述。**12–13 min：**不看稿说：

> **The change is ___. It affects ___. ___ needs to check ___. For ___, this is just FYI. I'll post a short update in ___.**

**loop in** 是把相关人带入沟通，不是把所有人都 @ 一遍。工程变更是否涉及其他团队，要根据真实影响判断。

### 13–15 min | Scene 3：PR 可能延期，提前报告而不是沉默

**Engineer:** Will your pull request be ready for review today?

**You:** I wanted to give you a heads-up: I found an edge case that needs another check.

**Engineer:** Is the feature blocked?

**You:** The main flow works, but I haven't finished verifying the failure case.

**Engineer:** Do you need anything from me?

**You:** No action needed yet. I'll share a reviewable version once the check is complete, and I'll update the ticket before 5 p.m.

**13–14 min：**朗读，注意 **reviewable**（可供评审）不等于 **approved**（已批准）。**14–15 min：**40 秒复述 **current state → risk → no action → next update**，最后 20 秒重读 **No action needed yet. I'll keep you posted.**

### 15–17 min | Timed oral substitution：12 轮 × 10 秒

**15:00–15:40｜A. 提前告知变化（4 轮）**

模板：**Just a heads-up: ___ may affect ___.**

1. the API delay / QA testing
2. the new design / the UI estimate
3. the meeting change / our demo
4. the missing test data / verification

**15:40–16:20｜B. 说清对方要不要行动（4 轮）**

模板：**For now, ___. I'll keep you posted.**

1. no action is needed
2. please hold off on the upload tests
3. please check the updated ticket
4. please don't change the invite yet

**16:20–17:00｜C. 条件与期限（4 轮）**

模板：**If ___, I should be able to ___ ___.**

1. the contract arrives by noon / update QA / by 1
2. the review ends on Monday / send a summary / on Tuesday
3. the design is approved today / finish the UI / before Friday
4. the logs arrive by Thursday / share the findings / on Friday

每轮 **3 秒看提示 + 7 秒完整大声说**，总计 120 秒。**by Friday** = 最迟周五；**on Friday** = 周五当天；**before Friday** = 周五之前。

## 17–23 min | Colleague Small Talk & Socializing

### 17–20 min | Scene 4：约午饭但会议延长，提前发消息

**Colleague:** We're heading to the food court at 12:30. Coming?

**You:** I'd love to. Just a heads-up: my meeting is running over.

**Colleague:** How much longer do you need?

**You:** Maybe ten minutes, but I'm not sure. Please go ahead without me.

**Colleague:** Okay, we'll send the location.

**You:** Thanks! If I finish before 12:45, I'll join you there. I'll keep you posted.

**17–18 min：**朗读两轮。**18–19 min：**换成 **coffee / another call / 3:15**。**19–20 min：**40 秒脱稿说 **friendly interest → reason → don't make others wait → conditional plan**；20 秒复读。

**My meeting is running over** = 我的会议超时了；**go ahead without me** = 你们不用等我。语气友好，不需要长篇解释。

### 20–23 min | Scene 5：周末聚会地点改变，通知而不是重新邀请

**Colleague:** Are we still meeting at the same café on Saturday?

**You:** Just a heads-up: the organiser is checking another place.

**Colleague:** Has the venue changed already?

**You:** Not yet. The original place is still our plan until the organiser confirms.

**Colleague:** Okay, do I need to reply again?

**You:** No action needed right now. We'll share the confirmed location in the group chat.

**20–21 min：**朗读。**21–22 min：**替换 **café → badminton court**、**Saturday → Sunday**。**22–23 min：**40 秒说 **proposed change → not confirmed → no action → update channel**；20 秒检查 **may / has** 的区别。

## 23–30 min | Restaurant / Shopping / Ride-hailing

### 23–25 min | Restaurant：原本选择的配菜没有了

**Staff:** Just to let you know, that side dish is unavailable now.

**You:** Thanks for the heads-up. Has my order been prepared yet?

**Staff:** Not yet. Would you like to choose another side?

**You:** Yes, could you show me the available options before changing the order?

**Staff:** Of course.

**You:** Thank you. I'll confirm my choice before you proceed.

**23–24 min：**朗读。**24–25 min：**替换 **side dish → drink**，练 **acknowledge → check status → choose → confirm**。店铺能否更改订单以实际情况为准。

### 25–27 min | Shopping：商品取货时间延后

**Staff:** Just a heads-up: the item may not be ready for collection today.

**You:** Thanks for letting me know. Is the new collection date confirmed?

**Staff:** Not yet. Please check the order updates through our official channel.

**You:** Understood. Could you notify me when the item is actually ready?

**Staff:** Please follow the notification options provided with your order.

**You:** Thanks. I won't make another trip until I have confirmation.

**25–26 min：**朗读。**26–27 min：**换成 **repair pickup / collection time**，练 **new information → confirmed or not → notification → next step**。

### 27–30 min | Ride-hailing：行程预计时间变化

**Driver:** Just to let you know, traffic is heavier than expected.

**You:** Thanks for the update. Does the app show a later arrival time?

**Driver:** Please check the latest estimate in the app.

**You:** I see. I'll let the person I'm meeting know that I may arrive later.

**Driver:** Okay.

**You:** If the ETA changes again, I'll use the app to check it. No need to message while driving.

**27–28 min：**分角色读两轮。**28–29 min：**换成 **road closure / alternative route**，练 **acknowledge → verify ETA → notify the person affected**。**29–30 min：**40 秒脱稿向等你的人发送口头消息，20 秒自检。不要要求司机边开车边操作手机。

## 30–35 min | Practical Singapore Errands

### 30–33 min | Scene 9：银行申请进度有变化，区分通知与批准

**You:** Hi, I received an update about my account application, but I'm not sure whether any action is required.

**Support:** Please check the exact status and instructions in the bank's official channel.

**You:** Understood. Does the message mean I need to submit another document, or is it just a status update?

**Support:** Please follow the instructions shown for your application.

**You:** Thanks. I'll check the required action before uploading anything.

**Support:** That's the right place to start.

**You:** If the status is unclear, I'll contact official support. I won't assume the account is approved.

**30–31 min：**朗读。**31–32 min：**按 **notification → action required? → official instructions → safe next step** 复述。**32–33 min：**换成 **debit card request**，说三句：**I received an update. / Is any action required from me? / Where can I verify the status?** 银行材料及批准状态以官方信息为准。

### 33–35 min | Scene 10：手机服务尚未生效，给客服提供简短状态更新

**You:** Hi, just a quick update on my mobile data issue. Calls are working, but mobile data still isn't connecting.

**Support:** Have you followed the official troubleshooting instructions?

**You:** Yes, I've checked the steps shown in the provider's app. I'm still waiting for the service status to update.

**Support:** Please use the official support channel if the problem continues.

**You:** Understood. If the status changes, I'll check the connection again and share the result.

**33–34 min：**朗读。**34–35 min：**40 秒脱稿说 **what works → what still fails → what you've checked → next update**；20 秒自检。只在官方渠道报告必要信息，不提供密码或 OTP。

## 35–37 min | Accurate Grammar Corrections

**35–36 min｜先自己改三句，再核对：**

1. ❌ **Just heads-up: the meeting may move.** → ✅ **Just a heads-up: the meeting may move.**（这里 heads-up 是单数可数名词，需要 a）
2. ❌ **I'll keep you update.** → ✅ **I'll keep you updated.** / ✅ **I'll keep you posted.**（用 updated / posted 表示持续告知）
3. ❌ **Please let know me if it changes.** → ✅ **Please let me know if it changes.**（固定语序 let + 人 + know）

**36–37 min｜复习两句：**

4. ❌ **I'm still waiting the confirmation.** → ✅ **I'm still waiting for confirmation.**
5. ❌ **If I will get the contract today, I should able to reply by Friday.** → ✅ **If I get the contract today, I should be able to reply by Friday.**（普通未来 if 从句用一般现在时；should be able to）

**正确替代表达，不算错误：** **Heads up!** 可以独立使用，不需要 a；**Just a heads-up / Just to let you know** 都自然；**I'll keep you posted / I'll keep you updated** 都正确；**I'll update you by Friday / on Friday / before Friday** 都正确，期限含义不同。

## 38–40 min | Final Speaking Challenges

### 38–39 min | Workplace：40 秒口述 + 15 秒参考核对 + 5 秒自检

提示：**API contract not final → upload tests affected → QA can continue unrelated tests → confirmation pending → if noon → update by 1**。

**38:00–38:40：**脱稿向 QA 解释变化。**38:40–38:55：**快速对照下面的参考措辞，重点看影响和行动要求。**38:55–39:00：**自检有没有过度承诺。

> **Just a heads-up: the API contract isn't final yet, so the upload tests may be affected. Could you hold off on those cases and continue the unrelated checks? I'm still waiting for confirmation. If it arrives by noon, I should be able to update you by 1 p.m.**

### 39–40 min | Everyday：40 秒口述 + 15 秒参考核对 + 5 秒自检

提示：**lunch meetup → meeting runs over → about ten minutes late → don't wait → if before 12:45 join → update**。

**39:00–39:40：**脱稿向同事解释。**39:40–39:55：**快速对照参考句。**39:55–40:00：**自检是否礼貌且留下了下一步。

> **Just a heads-up: my meeting is running over, so I may be about ten minutes late. Please go ahead without me. If I finish before 12:45, I'll join you at the food court. I'll keep you posted. Thanks!**

**40 分钟完成。今天最重要的一句：Just a heads-up: ___. This may affect ___. No action needed yet—I'll keep you posted.**
