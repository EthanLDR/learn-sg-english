## Day 5｜40 分钟新加坡英语口语训练

今天的主题是：**“请别人帮忙、交接工作、认识新同事、以及处理付款/退换/配送问题。”**  
重点是把这些表达练到你在新加坡办公室和生活里可以直接脱口而出。

---

## 0–10 分钟｜工作里请别人帮忙

### 场景：你需要同事帮你看一下代码

A: Hey, do you have a minute?  
B: Sure. What’s up?  
A: Could you take a quick look at this PR when you get a chance?  
B: Yeah, of course. Anything specific you want me to check?  
A: Mainly the error handling. I’m not sure if I’ve covered all the edge cases.  
B: Got it. I’ll have a look after lunch.

这里最重要的是 5 个表达。

### 1. Do you have a minute?

不是严格问“你有一分钟吗”，而是：

**现在方便聊一下吗？**

比如：

> Do you have a minute? I need your opinion on something.

比直接说：

> Help me with this.

自然很多。

---

### 2. Could you take a quick look at…?

非常适合工程师。

> Could you take a quick look at this PR?

> Could you take a quick look at this bug?

> Could you take a quick look at the logs?

---

### 3. when you get a chance

= **你方便的时候**

> Could you review this when you get a chance?

> Could you send me the link when you get a chance?

这个表达能让你的请求听起来没那么强硬。

---

### 4. Anything specific you want me to check?

= **有什么特别需要我检查的吗？**

你也可以反过来用：

> Could you check the error handling?

> Could you focus on the mobile layout?

---

### 5. I’ll have a look.

= **我看一下。**

非常自然。

类似：

> I’ll take a look.

> Let me have a look.

---

### 口语 Drill

连续说 3 轮：

> Do you have a minute?

> Could you take a quick look at this?

> When you get a chance.

> I’m mainly worried about the error handling.

第二轮把内容替换成：

> performance  
> API response  
> mobile layout  
> deployment logs

---

## 10–17 分钟｜工作交接：我今天不在，事情怎么办

### 场景：你明天请假

A: I’ll be off tomorrow, so I wanted to hand this over before I leave.  
B: Sure. What’s the current status?  
A: The frontend is done. We’re just waiting for QA to finish testing.  
B: Is there anything I need to keep an eye on?  
A: Yes, mainly the payment flow. If anything comes up, the details are in the ticket.  
B: Got it.

高频表达：

### I’ll be off tomorrow.

= **我明天休假 / 不上班。**

不要说：

❌ Tomorrow I don’t work.

更自然：

✅ I’ll be off tomorrow.

---

### I wanted to hand this over…

**hand over = 交接**

> I wanted to hand this over before I leave.

> Can we do a quick handover?

---

### What’s the current status?

= **现在进度怎么样？**

非常适合项目交接。

---

### Keep an eye on…

= **留意一下**

> Could you keep an eye on the deployment?

> Please keep an eye on the error rate.

---

### If anything comes up…

= **如果发生什么情况**

> If anything comes up, just message me.

> If anything comes up, check with Jason.

这个句型非常值得记。

---

## 17–23 分钟｜认识新同事：别像面试一样聊天

### 场景：公司来了一个新同事

A: Hey, I don’t think we’ve met. I’m Ethan.  
B: Nice to meet you. I’m Daniel.  
A: Which team are you on?  
B: I just joined the payments team.  
A: Nice. How are you finding it so far?  
B: Pretty good. Everyone’s been really helpful.

这里的重点不是：

> What is your job?

而是更自然地聊。

### I don’t think we’ve met.

= **我们好像还没见过。**

这是特别自然的开场。

---

### Which team are you on?

= **你在哪个团队？**

比：

> Which team do you belong to?

自然很多。

---

### How are you finding it so far?

这是很值得学的一句。

意思不是：

“你怎么找到它？”

而是：

**目前感觉怎么样？**

比如新公司、新加坡、新项目都能问：

> How are you finding Singapore so far?

> How are you finding the new team?

回答：

> Pretty good so far.

> It’s been good.

> Still getting used to it.

---

### Still getting used to it.

= **还在适应。**

比如：

> I’m still getting used to the weather.

> I’m still getting used to the commute.

> I’m still getting used to the new codebase.

---

## 23–28 分钟｜同事闲聊：午饭时自然延续话题

### 场景

A: How long have you been in Singapore?  
B: About six months.  
A: How are you liking it?  
B: I like it a lot. The only thing I’m still getting used to is the weather.  
A: Yeah, the humidity takes some getting used to.

重点：

### How long have you been…?

> How long have you been in Singapore?

> How long have you been on this team?

> How long have you been working here?

回答：

> About six months.

> Just over a year.

> Almost two years.

---

### takes some getting used to

= **需要一点时间适应**

> The weather takes some getting used to.

> The accent takes some getting used to.

> The commute takes some getting used to.

---

## 28–33 分钟｜购物 / 付款：刷卡失败、换支付方式

### 场景

Staff: Sorry, your payment didn’t go through.  
You: Oh, let me try again.  
Staff: Sure.  
You: It’s still not working. Can I pay by PayNow instead?  
Staff: Yes, that’s fine.

新加坡生活里非常实用。

### My payment didn’t go through.

= **付款没成功。**

你也可以说：

> My card isn’t going through.

---

### Let me try again.

> Let me try again.

> Let me use another card.

> Let me check my banking app.

---

### Can I pay by…?

> Can I pay by card?

> Can I pay by PayNow?

> Can I pay in cash?

注意：

❌ Can I pay with cash?

这其实也对。

日常里：

✅ pay by card  
✅ pay in cash  
都很常见。

---

## 33–36 分钟｜退换货：东西有问题

### 场景

You: Hi, I bought this a few days ago, but it doesn’t seem to be working properly.  
Staff: Do you have the receipt?  
You: Yes, here it is. Could I exchange it for a new one?  
Staff: Sure, let me check.

重点：

### It doesn’t seem to be working properly.

比：

> It is broken.

更柔和、更自然。

---

### Could I exchange it for…?

> Could I exchange it for a new one?

> Could I exchange it for a different size?

---

### Do you have the receipt?

一定会听到。

receipt = 小票 / 收据

---

## 36–38 分钟｜配送 / 公寓：快递没收到

### 场景

You: Hi, I’m calling about a delivery.  
Staff: What seems to be the issue?  
You: The app says it was delivered, but I haven’t received it.  
Staff: Have you checked with the reception?  
You: Yes, but they don’t have it either.

特别实用：

> The app says it was delivered.

> I haven’t received it.

> I’ve already checked with the reception.

### check with someone

= **跟某人确认**

> I’ll check with the driver.

> Let me check with my manager.

> Could you check with the delivery team?

## 38–40 分钟｜Grammar Fix + 今日复习

今天纠正 6 个常见错误。

❌ Can you help me to see this PR?  
✅ **Could you take a look at this PR?**

❌ Tomorrow I will take a holiday.  
✅ **I’ll be off tomorrow.**

❌ Please attention this issue.  
✅ **Please keep an eye on this issue.**

❌ How do you feel Singapore?  
✅ **How are you finding Singapore so far?**

❌ I still adapt Singapore.  
✅ **I’m still getting used to Singapore.**

❌ My payment is failed.  
✅ **My payment didn’t go through.**
