## Day 21｜40-minute Singapore English Speaking Lesson  
### Theme: Clarifying assumptions, avoiding misunderstandings, and keeping work moving

今天重点练：**需求不清楚时怎么确认、如何避免误解、怎么表达“我还在等”、如何给一个不冒进的完成时间**，同时覆盖同事闲聊、餐厅、购物、Grab 和新加坡日常办事。

---

## 0–5 min｜Warm-up：先把几个核心句型说顺

大声读 3 遍：

> **Let me make sure we’re on the same page.**  
> 我确认一下我们理解是不是一致。

> **I’m still waiting for the backend API.**  
> 我还在等后端 API。

> **If I get it by noon, I should be able to finish by Friday.**  
> 如果我中午前拿到，我应该能在周五前完成。

> **Before we move on, can I clarify one thing?**  
> 在继续之前，我能确认一件事吗？

今天要特别复习：

- **by Friday** = 最迟周五完成
- **on Friday** = 周五当天发生
- **before Friday** = 周五之前，不包括周五
- **If + 一般现在时**, 主句再用 `will / should / can`

✅  
> If I get the API today, I should be able to finish by Friday.

不要说：

❌  
> If I will get the API today...

# 5–17 min｜Office Communication & Meetings

## 场景 1：需求听起来有歧义

**PM:**  
We want users to be able to edit this after submission.

**You:**  
Just to clarify, do you mean they can edit everything, or only certain fields?

**PM:**  
Only certain fields.

**You:**  
Got it. And should those changes take effect immediately?

**PM:**  
Yes.

这里的核心句型：

> **Just to clarify, do you mean A or B?**

非常适合需求澄清。

替换练习：

> Just to clarify, do you mean the mobile app or the web app?

> Just to clarify, do you mean all users or only admins?

> Just to clarify, do you mean before submission or after submission?

---

## 场景 2：确认自己的理解

你可以说：

> **Let me make sure I’ve understood this correctly.**

然后自己总结：

> Users can edit the address after submission, but they can’t change the payment method. Is that right?

最后：

> **Is that right?**

或者：

> **Have I got that right?**

都很自然。

---

## 场景 3：依赖还没到，怎么说 deadline

**Manager:**  
Are we still on track for Friday?

**You:**  
I think so. I’m still waiting for the backend API, though.

**Manager:**  
When do you expect to get it?

**You:**  
They said it should be ready by noon.

**Manager:**  
Will that give you enough time?

**You:**  
If I get it by noon, I should be able to finish the integration by Friday.

这段非常适合你反复练。

核心结构：

> **I’m still waiting for ___, though.**

> **If I get ___ by ___, I should be able to ___ by ___.**

替换：

> If I get the design by this afternoon, I should be able to finish the UI by tomorrow.

> If the API is ready by Wednesday, we should be able to test it before Friday.

---

## 场景 4：你觉得大家理解不一致

你可以说：

> **I think there may be a misunderstanding here.**

然后不要直接说：

> You misunderstood me.

更柔和：

> **I may not have explained it clearly.**

或者：

> **I think we may be talking about two different things.**

完整表达：

> I think we may be talking about two different things. I’m referring to the API response, not the UI behaviour.

非常自然。

---

## 2-minute Oral Substitution Drill

下面每句说 3 遍，每次换一个词。

### Pattern 1

> I’m still waiting for **the API**.

替换：

- the design
- approval
- the test results
- feedback from the backend team

### Pattern 2

> I should be able to finish it **by Friday**.

替换：

- by tomorrow
- by the end of the day
- before the meeting
- by next Monday

### Pattern 3

> Before we move on, can I clarify **one thing**?

替换：

- the requirement
- the timeline
- the expected behaviour
- who owns this task

# 17–24 min｜Colleague Small Talk & Socializing

## 场景 1：同事问你最近怎么样

**Colleague:**  
How’s your week going?

你：

> Pretty good. It’s been a bit busy, but nothing too crazy.

同事：

> Same here.

你：

> I’m looking forward to the weekend already.

很好用：

> **I’m looking forward to the weekend.**

注意：

`look forward to` 后面如果接动词，要用 **-ing**：

> I’m looking forward to seeing everyone.

不是：

> ❌ looking forward to see

---

## 场景 2：午饭随口聊天

**Colleague:**  
Have you tried the food court downstairs?

你：

> Not yet. Is it any good?

同事：

> Yeah, the Japanese place is pretty decent.

你：

> Nice. I might give it a try today.

这里：

> **Is it any good?**

不是“它有任何好吗”，而是：

**怎么样？好不好？**

非常口语。

---

## 场景 3：同事约你喝咖啡

**Colleague:**  
We’re going for coffee. You coming?

你：

> Yeah, give me two minutes.

或者：

> I’ll join you in a bit.

如果不去：

> I’ll pass this time. I’ve got a meeting in ten minutes.

# 24–31 min｜Restaurant / Shopping / Ride-hailing

## 餐厅：确认订单

服务员重复订单，你发现不对：

> Sorry, I think there’s been a misunderstanding.

然后：

> I ordered the chicken, not the beef.

或者更柔和：

> I think I ordered the chicken.

如果你怕自己记错：

> Could you double-check the order for me?

---

## 餐厅：确认时间

> How long will the food take?

更自然一点：

> **Roughly how long will it take?**

如果赶时间：

> I need to leave by 1:30. Do you think that’ll be enough time?

这里：

> **leave by 1:30**

= 最迟 1:30 必须离开。

---

## Shopping：问是否可以晚点回来买

> I’m still deciding.

> Could you hold this for me until this evening?

或者：

> I’ll come back later if I decide to get it.

注意 future `if`：

✅  
> If I decide to get it, I’ll come back later.

不是：

❌  
> If I will decide...

---

## Grab：司机找不到你

**Driver:**  
Where are you?

你：

> I’m at the main entrance, next to the taxi stand.

司机：

> I don’t see you.

你：

> I might be at the wrong pickup point. Give me a second.

然后：

> I’ll walk over to Pickup Point B.

非常自然：

> **Give me a second.**

= 等我一下。

# 31–36 min｜Practical Singapore Life Errands

## 场景 1：银行 / 柜台确认材料

> I just want to make sure I have everything I need.

然后：

> Do I need to bring the original documents?

> Is a digital copy okay?

> Do I need to submit this before Friday?

---

## 场景 2：物业维修

你已经报修，但是还没人来：

> Hi, I’m just following up on a maintenance request.

工作人员：

> When did you report it?

你：

> I reported it on Tuesday, but I’m still waiting for someone to come and check it.

复习：

> **on Tuesday**  
> 周二当天

> **by Tuesday**  
> 最迟周二

> **before Tuesday**  
> 周二之前

---

## 场景 3：确认下一步

工作人员说完一堆流程后，你可以总结：

> So I need to submit the form first, and then wait for the confirmation email. Is that correct?

或者：

> Just to make sure, what do I need to do next?

这类句子在新加坡办银行、物业、政府服务时非常实用。

# 36–38 min｜Common Grammar Fixes

今天快速纠正 5 个容易犯的问题。

❌ `I am still wait for the API.`  
✅ **I’m still waiting for the API.**

结构：

> be + waiting

---

❌ `I should can finish it by Friday.`  
✅ **I should be able to finish it by Friday.**

`should` 和 `can` 不能这样直接放一起。

---

❌ `I will finish it until Friday.`  
如果你想表达“最迟周五完成”：

✅ **I’ll finish it by Friday.**

`until Friday` 更像“持续到周五”。

---

❌ `If the API will be ready today, we can test tomorrow.`  
✅ **If the API is ready today, we can test tomorrow.**

Future `if` 条件句里通常用一般现在时。

---

❌ `I want to confirm with you one thing.`

能理解，但更自然：

✅ **I just want to confirm one thing with you.**

或者：

✅ **Can I clarify one thing?**

---

# 38–40 min｜Final Speaking Challenge

先读一遍：

> Before we move on, I just want to make sure we’re on the same page. I’m still waiting for the backend API. They said it should be ready by noon. If I get it by then, I should be able to finish the integration by Friday. If there are any further changes, though, we may need to adjust the timeline.

第二遍只看关键词：

**same page → waiting → API → noon → if → should be able to → Friday → changes → timeline**

第三遍完全脱稿。

# Day 21 Compact Review

| 场景 | 直接说 |
|---|---|
| 确认理解一致 | **Let me make sure we’re on the same page.** |
| 澄清需求 | **Just to clarify, do you mean A or B?** |
| 总结理解 | **Have I got that right?** |
| 还在等待 | **I’m still waiting for the API.** |
| 最迟周五 | **by Friday** |
| 周五当天 | **on Friday** |
| 周五之前 | **before Friday** |
| 有条件地承诺 | **If I get it by noon, I should be able to finish by Friday.** |
| 可能有误解 | **I think there may be a misunderstanding.** |
| 自己没讲清楚 | **I may not have explained it clearly.** |
| 饭店怎么样 | **Is it any good?** |
| 跟进办事 | **I’m just following up on...** |

今天最值得练到脱口而出的 5 句：

> **Let me make sure we’re on the same page.**

> **I’m still waiting for the backend API.**

> **If I get it by noon, I should be able to finish by Friday.**

> **Before we move on, can I clarify one thing?**

> **I may not have explained it clearly.**
