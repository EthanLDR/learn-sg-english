## Day 18｜40-minute Singapore English Speaking Lesson

今天重点练一组你在新加坡工作和生活里很常见、但前面没有系统展开过的场景：**会议里表达不确定、推进决策、确认依赖、同事临时邀约、餐厅排队与订位、购物询问库存、Grab 临时改上车点，以及日常办事时确认规则和资格**。

目标还是一样：**说得自然、短、能直接用。**

### 0–5 min｜Warm-up：不确定时怎么说

先大声读 3 遍：

> **I’m not completely sure yet. Let me verify that first.**  
> 我现在还不能完全确定，我先确认一下。

> **There are still a couple of things we need to sort out.**  
> 还有几件事情需要处理清楚。

> **Can we make a decision on this today?**  
> 这件事我们今天可以定下来吗？

> **I’m waiting on one dependency before I can move forward.**  
> 我还在等一个依赖，之后才能继续推进。

今天先记一个很好用的区别：

> **I’m waiting for...**  
> 我在等某个人/某个东西。

> **I’m waiting on...**  
> 职场口语里也常表示“还依赖这个东西，等它完成”。

例如：

> I’m waiting on the backend team.

> I’m waiting on approval.

> I’m waiting on the final design.

## 5–17 min｜Office Communication & Meetings

### 场景 1：你现在还不能给确定答案

**Manager:**  
Will this fix the issue?

**You:**  
I think so, but I’m not completely sure yet.

**Manager:**  
What do you need to check?

**You:**  
I need to verify how it behaves with older data.

**Manager:**  
Okay.

**You:**  
I’ll confirm once I’ve tested that case.

这里重点：

> **I think so, but I’m not completely sure yet.**

比直接说：

> I don’t know.

更专业、更自然。

替换练习：

> I think so, but I need to verify it.

> It should work, but I’d like to test it first.

> I believe so, but let me double-check.

---

### 场景 2：事情卡在依赖上

**PM:**  
Why hasn’t this started yet?

**You:**  
I’m waiting on the final API spec before I can move forward.

**PM:**  
Is that blocking you completely?

**You:**  
For this part, yes. I can work on something else in the meantime.

非常值得背：

> **before I can move forward**

= 在我能继续推进之前。

以及：

> **in the meantime**

= 与此同时 / 在等待期间。

例如：

> I’ll work on the UI in the meantime.

> We can test the other flow in the meantime.

---

### 场景 3：推进会议做决定

**You:**  
We’ve discussed a few options. Can we make a decision on this today?

**Manager:**  
Which option do you prefer?

**You:**  
I’d go with option A because it’s simpler and lower risk.

**Manager:**  
Any concerns?

**You:**  
The main concern is that we may need to revisit it later.

这里重点：

> **I’d go with option A.**

= 我会选 A。

比：

> I choose A.

自然很多。

还可以说：

> I’d lean toward option A.

`lean toward` = 稍微倾向于。

例如：

> I’m leaning toward the simpler solution.

---

### 场景 4：总结会议中的未解决问题

你可以说：

> **There are still two open questions.**

这里的：

> open question

不是“开放式问题”。

而是：

**还没有定论的问题。**

例如：

> We still have one open question around performance.

> The ownership is still an open question.

然后：

> Can we resolve that before tomorrow’s meeting?

---

## 17–24 min｜Colleague Small Talk & Socializing

### 场景 1：同事临时约咖啡

**Colleague:**  
We’re grabbing coffee. Want anything?

你可以说：

> Yeah, could you get me an iced Americano?

如果不需要：

> I’m good, thanks.

这里：

> **I’m good, thanks.**

在这种场景不是：

“我很好。”

而是：

**不用了，谢谢。**

非常常用。

---

### 场景 2：同事邀你临时吃饭

**Colleague:**  
A few of us are going out for dinner after work. Want to join?

你：

> Sounds good. What time are you guys heading out?

如果有事：

> I might be able to join a bit later.

或者：

> I’ve got something on tonight, so I’ll have to pass this time.

`I’ll have to pass`  
= 这次我就不参加了。

自然又礼貌。

---

### 场景 3：聊天气和通勤

**Colleague:**  
The rain was crazy this morning.

你：

> Yeah, I got caught in it on the way to work.

`get caught in the rain`  
= 被雨淋到。

再练：

> I got caught in traffic.

> I got stuck in traffic.

两者不一样：

> got caught in the rain = 被雨淋到

> got stuck in traffic = 堵车了

---

## 24–31 min｜Restaurant / Shopping / Ride-hailing

### 餐厅：排队

你问：

> **How long is the wait?**

店员：

> Around 20 minutes.

你：

> That’s fine. Can you put my name down?

`put my name down`  
= 把我的名字登记进等位名单。

如果想问有没有位置：

> Do you have a table for two?

如果没有订位：

> We don’t have a reservation. Is there any chance you have a table available?

---

### 餐厅：菜还没上

> Excuse me, we’re still waiting for one dish.

> Could you check on it for us?

这个：

> **check on it**

= 帮忙确认一下状态。

比：

> check it

更自然。

---

### Shopping：问库存

> **Do you have this in stock?**

= 这个有现货吗？

> Do you have this in my size?

> Do you have this in another colour?

如果没货：

> Do you know when it’ll be back in stock?

非常实用。

---

### Grab / Taxi：临时改上车点

你可以说：

> **Would it be easier if I meet you at the other entrance?**

司机：

> Yes, please.

你：

> Okay, I’ll walk over now.

或者：

> I’ll update the pickup point in the app.

这里：

> **Would it be easier if...?**

是非常自然、礼貌的协商句型。

---

## 31–36 min｜Practical Singapore Life Errands

### 场景 1：确认自己是否符合资格

比如银行、政府服务、会员、优惠：

> **Am I eligible for this?**

`eligible` = 符合资格。

例如：

> Am I eligible to apply?

> Am I eligible for this plan?

如果这个词太难，也可以说：

> Can I apply for this?

---

### 场景 2：确认规则

你可以问：

> **Is there any restriction on this?**

> Is there a limit?

> Is there a minimum amount?

> Is there an expiry date?

这几句特别适合：

银行、优惠券、会员、停车、手机套餐。

---

### 场景 3：提交材料

工作人员：

> You’ll need to submit these documents online.

你：

> Okay. Do I need to upload the originals?

如果不确定格式：

> Does it need to be in PDF format?

如果确认是否完成：

> How will I know once it’s been approved?

这句非常实用。

---

## 36–38 min｜Common Grammar Fixes

今天纠正 5 个高频问题。

❌ `I am not very sure.`  
虽然能听懂，但更自然：

✅ **I’m not completely sure.**

或者：

✅ **I’m not sure yet.**

---

❌ `I am waiting the approval.`  
✅ **I’m waiting for approval.**

或者职场里：

✅ **I’m waiting on approval.**

---

❌ `I prefer option A more.`  
✅ **I prefer option A.**

`prefer` 本身已经有“更偏好”的意思。

---

❌ `How long need to wait?`  
✅ **How long is the wait?**

或者：

✅ **How long do we need to wait?**

---

❌ `Do you have stock?`  
更自然：

✅ **Do you have this in stock?**

---

## 38–40 min｜Final Speaking Drill

先完整读：

> We still have a couple of open questions, and I’m waiting on one dependency before I can move forward. I’m leaning toward the simpler option, but I’d like to verify one thing first. If possible, I’d like us to make a decision today so we can stay on track.

第二遍只看关键词：

**open questions → dependency → move forward → simpler option → verify → decision → stay on track**

第三遍自己说，不要逐句翻译。

## Day 18 Compact Review

| 场景 | 最自然表达 |
|---|---|
| 还不确定 | **I’m not completely sure yet.** |
| 等依赖 | **I’m waiting on one dependency.** |
| 继续推进 | **before I can move forward** |
| 等的时候先做别的 | **in the meantime** |
| 选某方案 | **I’d go with option A.** |
| 倾向某方案 | **I’m leaning toward...** |
| 未解决问题 | **We still have two open questions.** |
| 不需要咖啡 | **I’m good, thanks.** |
| 这次不参加 | **I’ll have to pass this time.** |
| 等位多久 | **How long is the wait?** |
| 有现货吗 | **Do you have this in stock?** |
| 是否符合资格 | **Am I eligible for this?** |

今天最值得反复说的 4 句：

> **I’m not completely sure yet. Let me verify that first.**

> **I’m waiting on one dependency before I can move forward.**

> **I’d go with the simpler option.**

> **Can we make a decision on this today?**

这四句能明显提高你在会议里处理“不确定、依赖、方案选择和推进决策”的能力。
