# Day 23 | Clarifying ownership, escalating blockers without blame, and agreeing next steps

### Theme: 出问题时不甩锅，把事实、影响、负责人和下一步说清楚

今天重点练一个工程师高频场景：**任务卡住、依赖没到、问题跨团队时，怎么说明情况但不指责别人，并把事情继续推进。**

你会反复练这几个动作：

- 客观描述问题：**We’re blocked on the API response.**
- 避免甩锅：**I don’t think this is anyone’s fault. We just need to clarify the dependency.**
- 确认负责人：**Who’s taking the next step on this?**
- 说明影响：**If we don’t get it by noon, the timeline may slip.**
- 给可执行方案：**I should be able to keep moving if we use mock data for now.**

同时继续复习：**I’m still waiting... / should be able to / by / on / before / future if**。

---

## 0–5 min | Warm-up：把“说明阻塞但不甩锅”说顺

下面 5 句，每句大声说 3 遍。

> **I’m still waiting for the backend response.**  
> 我还在等后端返回。

> **We’re blocked on one dependency at the moment.**  
> 我们目前被一个依赖卡住了。

> **I don’t want to blame anyone; I just want to make sure we’re clear on the next step.**  
> 我不是想怪谁，我只是想确认下一步大家都清楚。

> **If we get the API by noon, I should be able to finish the integration by Friday.**  
> 如果中午前拿到 API，我应该能在周五前完成集成。

> **Who’s taking the next step on this?**  
> 这件事下一步由谁来跟？

快速复习：

- **by Friday** = 最迟周五完成
- **on Friday** = 周五当天发生
- **before Friday** = 周五之前，不包括周五

Future `if`：

> **If we get the response today, we can test tomorrow.**

不是：

> ❌ If we will get the response today...

# 5–17 min | Office Communication & Meetings

## 5–9 min | 场景 1：站会里说明 blocker，但不把责任甩给后端

**Manager:**  
Any blockers on your side?

**You:**  
Yes, one. I’m still waiting for the backend response for the new endpoint.

**Manager:**  
Is the backend team late?

**You:**  
They’re still working on it. I don’t think it helps to frame it as someone being late. The main issue is that the frontend can’t finish the integration until the response format is confirmed.

**Manager:**  
What can you do in the meantime?

**You:**  
I can keep moving with mock data. If the final response is close to the current contract, I should be able to finish quickly once it’s ready.

这里的核心是：**事实 → 影响 → 临时方案**。

> **The issue is that...**

> **The impact is that...**

> **In the meantime, I can...**

比下面这种更专业：

> The backend team is blocking me.

这句话不一定语法错，但在协作场景里容易显得在甩锅。

---

## 9–13 min | 场景 2：需要升级问题，但不是“告状”

**You:**  
I think we may need to escalate this dependency.

**PM:**  
What’s the risk?

**You:**  
If we don’t get the confirmed API contract by noon, we probably won’t have enough time to test before Friday.

**PM:**  
What do you need from me?

**You:**  
Could you help confirm who owns the final decision on the response format?

**PM:**  
Yes, I’ll check with the backend lead.

**You:**  
Thanks. Once that’s confirmed, I should be able to move forward.

这里的 **escalate** 不是“投诉”，而是把问题提到能解决它的人那里。

好用结构：

> **I think we may need to escalate this because...**

> **The risk is that...**

> **What I need is...**

---

## 13–15 min | 场景 3：会议结束前确认 owner 和 deadline

讨论完以后，不要只说：

> Okay, sounds good.

最好把责任和时间一起确认：

> **Just to confirm, I’ll update the frontend today, and Alex will confirm the API contract by noon. Is that right?**

然后继续：

> **If that’s confirmed by noon, I should be able to start integration this afternoon.**

如果 owner 还不明确：

> **Who’s owning this action item?**

> **Who should I follow up with if I don’t hear back by noon?**

这里的 `own` 在职场里经常表示“负责”。

---

## 15–17 min | 2-minute Oral Substitution Drill

不要默读，连续开口替换。

### Pattern 1

> **I’m still waiting for the API contract.**

替换：

- the design approval
- the test result
- the security review
- feedback from product

### Pattern 2

> **If we get it by noon, I should be able to finish by Friday.**

替换：

- by 3 p.m. / finish by tomorrow
- today / test on Thursday
- before lunch / release on Friday
- this afternoon / finish before the meeting

### Pattern 3

> **Who’s taking the next step on this?**

替换：

- Who’s owning this action item?
- Who should I follow up with?
- Who’s making the final decision?
- Who’s confirming the requirement?

# 17–23 min | Colleague Small Talk & Socializing

## 17–20 min | 场景 1：同事问你最近忙不忙

**Colleague:**  
You look busy today. Everything okay?

**You:**  
Yeah, all good. I’ve got a couple of things waiting on other teams, so I’m just trying to keep everything moving.

**Colleague:**  
That sounds familiar.

**You:**  
Yeah. Nothing serious, just a lot of coordination.

几个自然表达：

> **Everything’s okay. I’m just juggling a few things.**  
> 都没问题，只是同时在处理几件事。

> **Nothing serious.**  
> 没什么严重的。

> **I’m just trying to keep things moving.**  
> 我只是尽量让事情继续往前走。

---

## 20–23 min | 场景 2：同事约午饭，但你还在等一个回复

**Colleague:**  
We’re heading out for lunch. Want to join?

**You:**  
I do, but I’m still waiting for a quick reply. Give me five minutes?

**Colleague:**  
Sure. We’ll wait downstairs.

**You:**  
Thanks. If I don’t get a reply by then, I’ll just deal with it after lunch.

这里继续复习：

> **I’m still waiting for...**

> **If I don’t get a reply by then, I’ll...**

`by then` = 到那个时间点之前。

# 23–30 min | Restaurant / Shopping / Ride-hailing

## 23–25 min | Restaurant：订单迟迟没来，先确认情况，不直接抱怨

**You:**  
Hi, could you help me check on my order?

**Staff:**  
Sure. What did you order?

**You:**  
The chicken rice. I ordered about twenty minutes ago.

**Staff:**  
Sorry, let me check with the kitchen.

**You:**  
No problem. I just want to know roughly how much longer it’ll take.

好用表达：

> **Could you help me check on my order?**

> **Roughly how much longer will it take?**

语气比直接说：

> Why is my food so late?

更容易让对方配合。

---

## 25–27 min | Shopping：线上订单还没准备好

**You:**  
Hi, I’m here to collect an online order, but I’m still waiting for the pickup notification.

**Staff:**  
What’s your order number?

**You:**  
Here it is. If it isn’t ready yet, I can come back later.

**Staff:**  
It should be ready in about ten minutes.

**You:**  
Okay, that works for me.

核心句：

> **I’m still waiting for the pickup notification.**

> **If it isn’t ready yet, I can come back later.**

---

## 27–30 min | Grab：司机和你对上车点理解不一致

**Driver:**  
I’m at the entrance.

**You:**  
I’m at Entrance B. Are you at the main entrance?

**Driver:**  
Yes.

**You:**  
Got it. I think we’re at different entrances. I’ll walk over to the main entrance now.

**Driver:**  
Okay.

**You:**  
I should be there in about three minutes.

这里不要急着说：

> You’re at the wrong place.

更柔和：

> **I think we’re at different entrances.**

把问题描述成“我们理解不一致”，而不是直接说“你错了”。

# 30–35 min | Practical Singapore Life Errands

## 30–33 min | 场景 1：维修请求还没有人处理

**You:**  
Hi, I’m following up on a maintenance request. I’m still waiting for someone to come and check the air conditioner.

**Staff:**  
When did you submit the request?

**You:**  
On Thursday.

**Staff:**  
I see. We should be able to send someone tomorrow.

**You:**  
Okay. Do you know roughly what time they’ll come?

**Staff:**  
Before noon.

**You:**  
Great. If no one arrives by noon, who should I contact?

这段同时复习：

> **on Thursday** = 周四当天提交

> **before noon** = 中午之前

> **by noon** = 最迟中午

---

## 33–35 min | 场景 2：柜台人员说了很多步骤，你需要确认谁负责下一步

工作人员解释完流程后，你可以总结：

> **So I submit the form today, and then your team will review it before Friday. Is that correct?**

如果之后要等通知：

> **Who should I contact if I’m still waiting for confirmation by Friday?**

如果是你自己还需要做下一步：

> **Is there anything else I need to do before I leave?**

这类“确认 owner + next step”的能力，在银行、物业、运营商、政府服务场景都很好用。

---

# 35–38 min | Common Grammar Fixes

今天只纠正确实有问题的表达。

### 1

❌ **I’m waiting the backend reply.**  
✅ **I’m waiting for the backend reply.**

`wait for + 人 / 事物`。

---

### 2

❌ **If they will confirm it today, I can start tomorrow.**  
✅ **If they confirm it today, I can start tomorrow.**

Future `if` 条件从句通常用一般现在时。

---

### 3

❌ **Who is responsible to this task?**  
✅ **Who is responsible for this task?**

`responsible for` 是固定搭配。

也可以自然地说：

> **Who owns this task?**

---

### 4

❌ **I should can finish it by Friday.**  
✅ **I should be able to finish it by Friday.**

`should` 后不能直接再接 `can`。

---

### 5

❌ **I submitted it in Thursday.**  
✅ **I submitted it on Thursday.**

具体某一天用 `on`。

注意：下面这些本身都正确，只是意思不同：

> **I’ll finish it by Friday.** = 最迟周五完成  
> **I’ll work on it until Friday.** = 会一直做到周五  
> **I’ll release it on Friday.** = 周五当天发布

---

# 38–40 min | Final Speaking Challenge

## 第 1 分钟：Workplace Challenge

只看关键词，自己组织一段 45–60 秒的话：

**blocker → waiting → no blame → impact → by noon → mock data → owner → next step**

参考版本：

> We’re blocked on one dependency at the moment. I’m still waiting for the confirmed backend response, but I don’t want to frame this as anyone being at fault. The main impact is that we can’t finish the integration until the response format is confirmed. If we get it by noon, I should be able to finish by Friday. In the meantime, I can keep moving with mock data. Could we confirm who owns the final decision and who I should follow up with?

## 第 2 分钟：Everyday Challenge

关键词：

**maintenance request → on Thursday → still waiting → tomorrow → before noon → if → by noon → contact**

参考版本：

> Hi, I’m following up on a maintenance request I submitted on Thursday. I’m still waiting for someone to check the air conditioner. I was told someone should be able to come tomorrow before noon. If no one arrives by noon, who should I contact to follow up?
