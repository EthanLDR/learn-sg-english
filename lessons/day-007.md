## Day 7｜40 分钟新加坡英语口语训练

今天的主题是：**“给进度更新、说明卡点、礼貌追问、和同事聊天，以及处理日常服务问题。”**  
目标是让你在真实办公室和生活场景里，说得更自然、更简洁。

### 0–10 分钟｜Stand-up：汇报进度 + 说明 blocker

先大声读两遍。

A: How’s the feature coming along?  
B: Pretty well. I’ve finished the main flow, and I’m working on the edge cases now.  
A: Any blockers?  
B: One small one. I’m waiting for access to the test environment.  
A: Does that affect the timeline?  
B: Not yet. I should still be able to finish by tomorrow.

今天先练 5 个核心句型。

**1. How’s ___ coming along?**  
= ……进展怎么样？

> How’s the migration coming along?  
> How’s the bug fix coming along?  
> How’s the redesign coming along?

**2. I’ve finished ___, and I’m working on ___ now.**

> I’ve finished the UI, and I’m working on the API integration now.

这是 stand-up 里非常实用的模板。

**3. Any blockers?**  
= 有什么卡点吗？

回答：

> No blockers so far.

或者：

> One small blocker.

**4. I’m waiting for access to…**

> I’m waiting for access to the staging environment.

> I’m waiting for access to the dashboard.

**5. I should still be able to…**

> I should still be able to finish by tomorrow.

这个 still 很重要，表示：

**尽管有一点问题，但我应该还是可以……**

#### 口语替换练习

连续说三轮：

> I’ve finished the main flow.

> I’m working on the edge cases now.

> I’m waiting for access to staging.

> I should still be able to finish by tomorrow.

然后把内容替换成你自己的真实工作。

## 10–17 分钟｜会议：当你不确定对方的意思

场景：

A: We may need to change the implementation a little.  
B: In what way?  
A: We want to move some of the logic out of the client.  
B: Okay. When you say “some of the logic,” which part do you mean exactly?  
A: Mainly the permission checks.  
B: Got it. That makes sense.

今天重点练：

**In what way?**

= 哪方面？怎么改？

比：

> How?

更自然、更具体。

**When you say ___, what do you mean exactly?**

这个句型非常适合开会。

> When you say “simplify,” what do you mean exactly?

> When you say “high priority,” what do you mean exactly?

**Which part do you mean?**

> Which part do you mean exactly?

**That makes sense.**

= 明白了，这样说得通。

不要每次都只说：

> I understand.

#### Drill

连续说：

> In what way?

> What do you mean exactly?

> Which part do you mean?

> Got it. That makes sense.

这四句在会议里非常高频。

## 17–23 分钟｜礼貌追同事，不显得催得太凶

场景：

A: Hey, any update on the API change?  
B: I’m still working on it.  
A: No worries. Do you think you’ll have it by this afternoon?  
B: Yeah, I should.  
A: Great. Just ping me when it’s ready.

重点：

**Any update on ___?**

> Any update on the design?

> Any update on the ticket?

> Any update on the deployment?

**Do you think you’ll have it by ___?**

比：

> When will you finish?

更柔和。

**Just ping me when it’s ready.**

ping = 发个消息提醒我。

在 tech 公司里非常常见。

> Ping me on Slack.

> Just ping me when you’re done.

注意不是所有正式场合都用 ping，但同事之间很自然。

## 23–28 分钟｜同事闲聊：周五 / 周末对话

场景：

A: Got any plans for the weekend?  
B: Nothing major. I might check out a new cafe.  
A: Nice. Which area?  
B: Probably somewhere around Tiong Bahru.  
A: Good choice. There are loads of nice places there.

今天练这种自然回答，不要只说：

> No.

可以说：

> Nothing major.

= 没什么特别的。

> I might just take it easy.

= 可能就放松一下。

> I haven’t decided yet.

= 还没决定。

> I’m thinking of checking out…

**check out** = 去看看 / 体验一下

> I’m thinking of checking out a new restaurant.

> I’m thinking of checking out Gardens by the Bay.

#### 接话句型

> Nice. Which area?

> How did you hear about it?

> Is it any good?

> Let me know how it is.

最后一句特别自然：

**Let me know how it is.**  
= 到时候告诉我怎么样。

## 28–33 分钟｜餐厅：想换桌、补餐具、确认账单

场景：

You: Excuse me, would it be possible to move to that table?  
Staff: Sure, as long as it’s not reserved.  
You: Thanks. Also, could we get another set of cutlery?  
Staff: Of course.  
You: And could we have the bill when you get a chance?

高频句型：

**Would it be possible to…?**

> Would it be possible to move to another table?

**Could we get another…?**

> Could we get another plate?

> Could we get another glass?

> Could we get another set of cutlery?

cutlery = 刀叉餐具。

**when you get a chance**

又出现了，因为真的非常实用。

> Could we have the bill when you get a chance?

## 33–36 分钟｜Grab / Taxi：上车后确认目的地和入口

场景：

Driver: Going to Marina Bay Financial Centre?  
You: Yes, that’s right. Could you drop me off at the main lobby?  
Driver: Which tower?  
You: Tower 2, please.  
Driver: Okay.

你会经常用到：

> Yes, that’s right.

> Could you drop me off at the main lobby?

> The entrance on the left, please.

> This is fine, thanks.

如果已经到了附近：

> You can drop me off here.

这句非常实用。

## 36–38 分钟｜生活办事：维修人员迟到 / 预约没来

场景：

You: Hi, I had a maintenance appointment for 2 pm, but no one has arrived yet.  
Staff: Let me check.  
You: Do you know when the technician is expected to arrive?  
Staff: Around 2:30.  
You: Okay, thanks.

重点：

**no one has arrived yet**

不要说：

❌ Nobody didn’t come.

正确：

✅ Nobody came.

或者：

✅ No one has arrived yet.

**is expected to arrive**

= 预计到达。

> When is the technician expected to arrive?

> When is the delivery expected to arrive?

生活里非常好用。

## 38–40 分钟｜Grammar Fix + Compact Review

今天纠正 6 个常见错误。

❌ How is the project going now?  
✅ **How’s the project coming along?**

❌ I’m waiting access.  
✅ **I’m waiting for access.**

❌ What you mean exactly?  
✅ **What do you mean exactly?**

❌ Do you have update?  
✅ **Do you have an update?**  
更口语：**Any update?**

❌ I will go to see a cafe.  
✅ **I might check out a cafe.**

❌ Nobody didn’t arrive.  
✅ **Nobody arrived.**  
或：**No one has arrived yet.**

### 今日 Compact Review

今天只需要复习这 12 句：

> How’s it coming along?

> I’ve finished the main flow.

> I’m working on the edge cases now.

> Any blockers?

> I should still be able to finish by tomorrow.

> What do you mean exactly?

> Which part do you mean?

> Any update on this?

> Just ping me when it’s ready.

> Nothing major.

> I’m thinking of checking out a new cafe.

> When is the technician expected to arrive?

最后做一个 **2 分钟自由口语挑战**：

上午 stand-up：

> I’ve finished…  
> I’m working on…  
> One small blocker is…  
> I should still be able to…

会议里：

> What do you mean exactly?  
> Which part do you mean?

下午追进度：

> Any update on…?  
> Just ping me when…

和同事聊周末：

> Nothing major.  
> I’m thinking of checking out…

晚上处理维修：

> No one has arrived yet.  
> When is the technician expected to arrive?

今天最值得练成肌肉记忆的是这四句：

**How’s it coming along?**  
**I should still be able to…**  
**What do you mean exactly?**  
**Just ping me when it’s ready.**
