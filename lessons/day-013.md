今天这节继续沿用你昨天的表达，但换成更实用的新加坡工作与生活场景，重点练“能直接说出口”的英语。

## 40-minute Singapore English Speaking Lesson

### 1) Warm-up — 5 min

先大声读下面 4 句，每句 3 遍：

> I’m still working on it.  
> I should be able to finish it by this afternoon.  
> I’ll keep you posted.  
> Let me double-check and get back to you.

今天新增两个高频句：

**I’ll keep you posted.**  
= 有进展我会告诉你。

**Let me double-check and get back to you.**  
= 我再确认一下，然后回复你。

非常适合新加坡办公室和跨团队沟通。

快速替换练习：

> Let me double-check the API and get back to you.

> Let me double-check the numbers and get back to you.

> Let me double-check with the backend team and get back to you.

---

## 2) Office Communication & Meetings — 12 min

### 场景 A：Stand-up 汇报进度

**Manager:**  
Any updates from your side?

**You:**  
Yes. I finished the login flow yesterday. Today, I’m working on the payment page.

**Manager:**  
Any blockers?

**You:**  
Nothing major. I’m just waiting for the API response format to be confirmed.

**Manager:**  
Will that affect the timeline?

**You:**  
It shouldn’t. I should still be able to finish it by Friday.

重点句型：

> **Any updates from your side?**  
> 你这边有什么更新吗？

你可以回答：

> I finished ___ yesterday.

> Today, I’m working on ___.

> I’m currently waiting for ___.

> Nothing major.

> It shouldn’t affect the timeline.

工程师特别实用：

> **I’m blocked by...**

例如：

> I’m blocked by the backend API.

但在真实职场里，更柔和、更常见的是：

> **I’m waiting for the backend API.**

---

### 场景 B：会议里没听懂

不要硬装听懂。

自然说：

> Sorry, could you say that again?

更职场一点：

> Could you clarify what you mean by that?

或者：

> Just to make sure I understand correctly, are you saying we should delay the release?

这个句型非常值得背：

> **Just to make sure I understand correctly, are you saying... ?**

例如：

> Just to make sure I understand correctly, are you saying we need another round of testing?

> Just to make sure I understand correctly, are you saying this is a backend issue?

---

### 场景 C：你不同意，但不想太直接

不要直接：

> I disagree.

可以说：

> I see your point, but I have a slightly different view.

或者更简单：

> I’m not sure that will work.

> I think there might be another option.

工程会议里特别自然：

> **What if we... ?**

例如：

> What if we release this feature next week instead?

> What if we keep the current implementation for now?

大声练：

> I see your point, but I’m not sure that will work.

> What if we try a simpler approach?

---

## 3) Colleague Small Talk & Socializing — 7 min

### 场景：午饭

**Colleague:**  
Hey, have you had lunch?

**You:**  
Not yet. I was just about to go.

**Colleague:**  
We’re going downstairs. Want to join us?

**You:**  
Sure. Where are you guys going?

**Colleague:**  
Probably the food court.

**You:**  
Sounds good. Give me a minute.

几个非常自然的口语句：

> **I was just about to go.**  
> 我正准备去。

> **Want to join us?**  
> 要不要一起？

> **Sounds good.**  
> 可以啊 / 听起来不错。

> **Give me a minute.**  
> 等我一分钟。

---

### 同事问你新加坡生活

**Colleague:**  
How are you finding Singapore so far?

不要回答：

> Singapore is good.

更自然：

> I like it so far. I’m still getting used to the weather, though.

或者：

> So far, so good. I’m still figuring out where everything is.

很好用的句型：

> **I’m still getting used to...**

例如：

> I’m still getting used to the weather.

> I’m still getting used to the working style.

> I’m still getting used to speaking English every day.

注意：

❌ `I’m still get used to`

✅ `I’m still getting used to`

---

## 4) Restaurant / Shopping / Ride-hailing — 7 min

### 餐厅点餐

**Staff:**  
What would you like?

**You:**  
Can I get the grilled chicken?

**Staff:**  
Rice or noodles?

**You:**  
Rice, please. And could I have less rice?

**Staff:**  
Anything to drink?

**You:**  
Just water, thanks.

你可以反复套：

> Can I get ___?

> Could I have less ___?

> Could I have it without ___?

例如：

> Could I have it without onions?

> Could I have less sauce?

> Could I have the sauce on the side?

最后一句对减脂特别实用：

> **Could I have the sauce on the side?**

---

### Shopping

店员问：

> Can I help you?

你只是看看：

> I’m just looking, thanks.

问有没有别的尺寸：

> Do you have this in a larger size?

> Do you have this in black?

> Can I try this on?

---

### Grab / Taxi

司机：

> I’m here.

你：

> I’m coming down now.

如果司机找不到你：

> I’m at the lobby entrance.

> I’m standing next to the taxi stand.

> Could you wait for me for a minute?

如果交通堵：

> Is the traffic usually this bad around this time?

这句也很适合顺便和司机 small talk。

---

## 5) Practical Singapore Life Errands — 5 min

### 手机店

> Hi, I’d like to get a SIM card.

> Do you have a monthly plan?

> How much data does it include?

> Is there a contract?

万能结构：

> **I’d like to + 动词**

例如：

> I’d like to open an account.

> I’d like to change my address.

> I’d like to make an appointment.

---

### 公寓 / 前台

> Hi, I’m here to collect a parcel.

> Could you check if there’s a package for me?

如果维修：

> The air conditioner doesn’t seem to be working properly.

> Could someone come and take a look?

这个表达比：

> My air conditioner is broken.

稍微更自然，也不会说得太绝对。

---

## 6) Common Mistakes — 2 min

今天只记 4 个。

❌ `I will finish it until Friday.`  
✅ `I will finish it by Friday.`

**by Friday** = 最晚周五完成。

---

❌ `I very like Singapore.`  
✅ `I really like Singapore.`

---

❌ `I’m waiting the backend.`  
✅ `I’m waiting for the backend team.`

**wait for + 人/东西**

---

❌ `I didn’t understand what you say.`  
✅ `I didn’t understand what you said.`

因为说话已经发生了：

> what you **said**

---

## 7) Final Speaking Drill — 2 min

这段连续说 3 遍：

> Yesterday, I finished most of the frontend work. Today, I’m working on the payment flow. I’m still waiting for the backend team to confirm the API format, but it shouldn’t affect the timeline. If everything goes well, I should be able to finish it by Friday. I’ll keep you posted.

第二轮时，不看文字，只记关键词：

**yesterday → frontend → today → payment → backend → timeline → Friday → posted**

目标是靠“语块”说，不要逐字翻译。

## 今日复习卡

| 场景 | 最值得记的句子 |
|---|---|
| 汇报进度 | **I’m still working on it.** |
| 时间预估 | **I should be able to finish it by Friday.** |
| 继续同步 | **I’ll keep you posted.** |
| 没听懂 | **Could you clarify what you mean by that?** |
| 确认理解 | **Just to make sure I understand correctly...** |
| 委婉不同意 | **I see your point, but I’m not sure that will work.** |
| 午饭社交 | **I was just about to go.** |
| 适应新环境 | **I’m still getting used to it.** |
| 点餐 | **Could I have the sauce on the side?** |
| 办事 | **I’d like to...** |

今天最值得形成肌肉记忆的是这 3 句：

> **Let me double-check and get back to you.**  
> **Just to make sure I understand correctly...**  
> **I’ll keep you posted.**

这三句在新加坡办公室里使用频率非常高，而且能明显让你的英语听起来更自然、更职业。
