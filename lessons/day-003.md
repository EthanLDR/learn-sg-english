## Day 3｜40 分钟新加坡英语口语训练
今天的主题是：**推进工作、确认责任人、和同事约饭，以及处理 Grab / 快递 / 公寓报修。**

目标不是学很多新单词，而是把一些**非常高频的句型练到可以直接脱口而出**。

---

## 0–10 分钟｜会议：确认谁负责、什么时候完成

### 场景

A: Who’s taking care of the frontend changes?  
B: I can take that.  
A: Great. When do you think you can have it ready?  
B: I should be able to finish it by tomorrow afternoon.  
A: Sounds good. Could you update us once it’s on staging?  
B: Sure, I’ll drop a message in the group.

这里有 5 个非常实用的表达。

### 1. Who’s taking care of ___?

意思是：

**谁负责这个？**

比：

> Who will do this?

更自然。

例如：

> Who’s taking care of the API changes?

> Who’s taking care of the deployment?

> Who’s taking care of this bug?

---

### 2. I can take that.

意思不是“我可以拿那个”。

而是：

**这个我来负责。**

会议里非常自然。

类似的还有：

> I can handle that.

> I’ll take care of it.

区别：

> I can take that.  
偏“这项任务我接。”

> I’ll take care of it.  
偏“交给我，我会处理。”

---

### 3. When do you think you can have it ready?

非常自然的 deadline 问法。

意思：

**你预计什么时候能弄好？**

可以替换：

> When do you think you can have the PR ready?

> When do you think you can have the design ready?

> When do you think you can have the fix ready?

---

### 4. I should be able to…

这个句型特别值得记。

它比：

> I can finish it tomorrow.

稍微留了一点余地。

例如：

> I should be able to finish it today.

> I should be able to send it over by 3.

> I should be able to join the meeting.

---

### 5. Once it’s ready…

**once = 一旦 / 等……之后**

例如：

> I’ll let you know once it’s ready.

> I’ll message you once I’ve finished.

> We can test it once it’s deployed.

---

### 口语 Drill

连续读三轮：

> I can take that.

> I should be able to finish it by tomorrow.

> I’ll let you know once it’s ready.

第二轮换内容：

> I can take the frontend changes.

> I should be able to finish them by Friday.

> I’ll let you know once they’re on staging.

第三轮自己换成真实工作任务。

---

## 10–17 分钟｜催进度，但不要显得很凶

工作里非常常见：

你在等别人。

不要一上来：

> Why haven’t you finished it?

更自然的是：

### Just checking in on…

> Just checking in on the API update.

意思是：

**想确认一下 API 进展。**

完整一点：

> Just checking in on the API update. Do you have an ETA?

ETA = estimated time of arrival

在软件公司里，也经常直接指：

**预计什么时候完成。**

例如：

> Do you have an ETA for the fix?

> Any ETA on this?

后者比较口语。

---

### Is this still on track for Friday?

意思：

**周五完成的计划还没变吧？**

非常自然。

> Is this still on track for tomorrow?

> Are we still on track for the release?

---

### No rush, but…

如果不是真的很急：

> No rush, but could you send it over when you get a chance?

**when you get a chance**

= 你方便的时候。

---

### 对话

A: Hey, just checking in on the login issue.  
B: Yeah, I’m still debugging it.  
A: No worries. Do you have an ETA?  
B: I should have a fix by this afternoon.  
A: Great. Just let me know when it’s ready.

### 今日非常实用的一句

> **Just let me know when it’s ready.**

以后不要总说：

> Tell me when you finish.

前者更自然。

# 17–24 分钟｜同事闲聊：约午饭、约咖啡

### 场景

A: Are you free for lunch later?  
B: Yeah, I should be. What time are you thinking?  
A: Maybe around twelve thirty?  
B: Works for me. Where do you want to go?  
A: There’s a new place downstairs. Want to try it?  
B: Sure, I’m up for that.

重点表达：

### Are you free for ___?

> Are you free for lunch?

> Are you free for coffee later?

> Are you free after work?

---

### What time are you thinking?

这句话非常口语。

不是一定要说：

> What time do you want to go?

可以直接：

> What time are you thinking?

---

### Works for me.

超级高频。

意思：

**我可以 / 这个时间没问题。**

比如：

A: How about 3 pm?

B:

> Works for me.

不要每次都：

> Okay.

---

### I’m up for that.

意思：

**可以啊，我愿意。**

例如：

> Want to grab coffee?

> Yeah, I’m up for that.

也可以说：

> Sounds good.

---

## 新加坡办公室里你可能听到

> Want to grab lunch?

= 一起吃午饭吗？

> Where are we eating?

= 我们去哪吃？

> Wanna go downstairs?

= 要不要下楼吃？

这些都很自然。

---

## 1 分钟口语挑战

自己连续说：

> Are you free for lunch later?

> What time are you thinking?

> Twelve thirty works for me.

> Where do you want to go?

> Yeah, I’m up for that.

目标：**不翻译，直接说出来。**

# 24–30 分钟｜餐厅：改订单、加单、买单

### 场景

You: Can I get the chicken rice, please?  
Staff: Sure.  
You: Could I have less rice and extra vegetables?  
Staff: Okay.  
You: And could I add a drink?  
Staff: Sure. Anything else?  
You: That’s all, thanks.

重点：

### Could I have less ___?

> Could I have less rice?

> Could I have less ice?

> Could I have less sugar?

---

### Could I get extra ___?

> Could I get extra vegetables?

> Could I get extra chilli?

> Could I get extra sauce?

---

### Could I add ___?

已经点完了又想加：

> Could I add a coffee?

> Could I add another drink?

---

### 买单

比较自然：

> Could we get the bill, please?

在新加坡你也会经常听到：

> Can we have the bill?

都可以。

---

## 常见错误

❌ Give me less rice.

语法没错，但偏直接。

更自然：

✅ Could I have less rice?

---

❌ I want add one coffee.

✅ I’d like to add a coffee.

或者：

✅ Could I add a coffee?

---

# 30–34 分钟｜Grab：临时改下车地点

### 场景

You: Hi, would it be possible to drop me off at the other entrance instead?  
Driver: Which entrance?  
You: The one near the MRT station.  
Driver: Sure, no problem.  
You: Thanks, I appreciate it.

非常实用：

### Would it be possible to…?

比较礼貌。

> Would it be possible to stop here?

> Would it be possible to drop me off at the next entrance?

日常更简单也可以：

> Could you drop me off here?

---

### the one near…

**the one** 可以避免重复名词。

例如：

> Which entrance?

> The one near the MRT station.

或者：

> Which building?

> The one next to the mall.

# 34–37 分钟｜生活办事：快递 + 公寓维修

## 快递

如果快递员打电话：

Courier:

> Hi, I have a delivery for you.

你可以说：

> I’m not home right now. Could you leave it at the reception?

或者：

> Could you leave it outside my door?

如果想问什么时候到：

> Roughly what time will you arrive?

**roughly = 大概**

---

## 公寓报修

> Hi, I’d like to report an issue with the air-conditioning.

工作人员：

> What seems to be the problem?

你：

> It’s running, but the room isn’t getting cold.

或者：

> It keeps turning off by itself.

非常值得记住：

### It keeps + ing

表示：

**它老是……**

> It keeps disconnecting.

Wi-Fi 老掉线。

> It keeps crashing.

软件老崩溃。

> It keeps making a strange noise.

一直有奇怪声音。

这个句型工作和生活都非常好用。

# 37–39 分钟｜今日 Grammar Fix

今天集中改 6 个中国人非常容易犯的错误。

### ①

❌ I will responsible for this.

✅ **I’ll be responsible for this.**

或者更自然：

✅ **I’ll take care of this.**

---

### ②

❌ When you will finish it?

✅ **When will you finish it?**

更柔和：

✅ **When do you think you can have it ready?**

---

### ③

❌ I will tell you when I will finish.

✅ **I’ll let you know when I finish.**

这里 when 后面通常不用 will。

---

### ④

❌ I’m waiting your update.

✅ **I’m waiting for your update.**

更自然：

✅ **I’m waiting for an update from you.**

---

### ⑤

❌ Let’s go to eat lunch.

✅ **Let’s go for lunch.**

或者：

✅ **Let’s grab lunch.**

---

### ⑥

❌ Can you send me when you finish?

✅ **Can you send it to me when you’re done?**

或者：

✅ **Just send it over when you’re done.**
