今天这节 40 分钟，我们换一个主题：**“听不清、不同意、临时改计划时，怎么自然地说英语”**。这些场景在新加坡工作和生活里特别常见，而且很适合练真正能用上的口语。

## 0–10 分钟｜会议里没听懂，怎么自然确认

先读两遍这段对话。

**场景：同事在解释一个技术方案**

A: We’re thinking of moving this logic to the backend.  
B: Sorry, just to clarify, do you mean all of the validation logic?  
A: Not all of it. Just the parts related to permissions.  
B: Got it. So the frontend will still handle the basic checks, right?  
A: Exactly.

中文意思：

A：我们考虑把这部分逻辑移到后端。  
B：不好意思，我确认一下，你是说所有校验逻辑吗？  
A：不是全部，只是跟权限相关的部分。  
B：明白了。所以基础检查还是前端处理，对吧？  
A：对。

今天先记住这 4 个会议句型：

**1. Sorry, just to clarify…**  
很自然，适合“确认我有没有理解对”。

> Sorry, just to clarify, are we changing the deadline?  
> Sorry, just to clarify, is this for the mobile app only?

**2. Do you mean…?**

> Do you mean we need to rebuild the whole page?  
> Do you mean this will affect existing users?

**3. So, if I understand correctly…**

> So, if I understand correctly, we need to finish this before Thursday.

**4. Is that right?**

> The API will be ready tomorrow. Is that right?

### 口语替换训练

连续说下面 4 句，不要停：

> Sorry, just to clarify, are we changing the scope?  
> Sorry, just to clarify, are we changing the deadline?  
> Sorry, just to clarify, are we changing the API contract?  
> Sorry, just to clarify, are we changing the design?

然后练：

> So, if I understand correctly, the backend team will handle this.

换成：

> the frontend team  
> the design team  
> the infra team  
> the product team

### 常见错误

❌ What do you mean about this?  
✅ What do you mean **by this**?

❌ I don’t understand what you said just now.  
✅ I didn’t quite catch that.

后者更自然，也没那么生硬。

## 10–18 分钟｜会议里不同意，但不想太强硬

在新加坡办公室，直接说：

> I disagree.

不是错，但有时会显得偏硬。

更自然的是：

> I’m not sure that’s the best approach.

或者：

> I see your point, but I’m a bit concerned about the timeline.

对话：

A: I think we should launch it this Friday.  
B: I see your point, but I’m a bit concerned about the testing time.  
A: What do you suggest?  
B: Maybe we can move it to Monday and give QA a bit more time.

重点句型：

**I see your point, but…**

> I see your point, but I’m worried about performance.

**I’m a bit concerned about…**

> I’m a bit concerned about the timeline.  
> I’m a bit concerned about the user experience.

**Maybe we can…**

> Maybe we can simplify the first version.  
> Maybe we can release it in two phases.

**What do you think about…?**

> What do you think about pushing it to next week?

### 快速输出

假设 PM 说：

> Let’s release this tomorrow.

你不同意。

试着说：

> I see your point, but I’m a bit concerned about ______.  
> Maybe we can ______ instead.

## 18–25 分钟｜和同事闲聊：自然一点，不要像面试

**场景：早上碰到同事**

A: Morning. How’s your day going?  
B: Pretty good. Just a bit sleepy. I stayed up too late last night.  
A: Same here. Busy week?  
B: Yeah, a little. Hopefully it’ll calm down after Wednesday.

重点表达：

**How’s your day going?**

比单纯的：

> How are you?

更像真实办公室闲聊。

回答不要只说：

> Fine.

可以说：

> Pretty good.  
> Not too bad.  
> A bit busy today.  
> Kind of tired, to be honest.

**Busy week?**

这种省略式问法很常见。

类似：

> Long day?  
> Late night?  
> Tough meeting?

### 同事约咖啡

A: I’m grabbing coffee. Want anything?  
B: Yeah, an iced latte would be great. Thanks.  
A: No problem.

注意：

**I’m grabbing coffee.**

不是“抓咖啡”，而是：

“我要去买杯咖啡。”

还能说：

> I’m grabbing lunch.  
> I’m grabbing a quick bite.

### 你来练

说 30 秒：

> Morning. I’m a bit ______ today because ______.  
> I’ve got ______ later.  
> Hopefully ______.

例如：

> Morning. I’m a bit tired today because I went to bed late. I’ve got two meetings later. Hopefully the afternoon will be quieter.

## 25–31 分钟｜餐厅和咖啡店：改单、加东西、听不懂

### 点咖啡

You: Can I get a flat white, please?  
Staff: Hot or iced?  
You: Iced, please. And could I get it without sugar?  
Staff: Sure.

句型：

**Could I get it without…?**

> Could I get it without sugar?  
> Could I get it without ice?  
> Could I get it without onions?

**Could I get extra…?**

> Could I get extra sauce?  
> Could I get extra chilli?

新加坡常见：

> chilli

英式拼写更常见，而不是美式的 “chili”。

### 没听清店员的话

你可以说：

> Sorry, could you say that again?

或者更简短：

> Sorry?

也可以：

> Sorry, I didn’t catch that.

这比假装听懂实用得多。

## 31–35 分钟｜Grab / Taxi：司机找不到你

对话：

Driver: Hi, I’m here.  
You: Hi, I’m at the pickup point near the main entrance.  
Driver: I don’t see you.  
You: I’m next to the taxi stand. I’m wearing a black T-shirt.  
Driver: Okay, I see you now.

重点：

**I’m at the pickup point.**

**I’m next to…**

> I’m next to the taxi stand.  
> I’m next to the lobby.  
> I’m next to the convenience store.

**Could you drop me off at…?**

> Could you drop me off at the main entrance?

### 常见错误

❌ I’m waiting you downstairs.  
✅ I’m waiting **for you** downstairs.

❌ I’m in the entrance.  
✅ I’m **at the entrance**.

## 35–38 分钟｜生活办事：电话卡、银行、物业

### 手机卡

> I’d like to change my mobile plan.

> Does this plan include roaming?

> Is there an activation fee?

### 银行

> I’d like to update my address.

> What documents do I need?

> How long does it usually take?

这个句型非常万能：

**How long does it usually take?**

你可以用于：

> visa application  
> bank transfer  
> account opening  
> phone activation  
> repair

### 公寓物业

> Hi, the air-conditioning in my room isn’t working properly.

> Could someone come and take a look?

这是非常自然的报修表达。

## 38–40 分钟｜今日纠错 + 复习

今天最容易错的 6 个地方：

❌ What do you mean about this?  
✅ What do you mean **by this**?

❌ I’m waiting you.  
✅ I’m waiting **for you**.

❌ I didn’t hear clearly.  
✅ I didn’t quite catch that.

❌ I concern about the deadline.  
✅ I’m concerned about the deadline.

❌ Can you repeat again?  
✅ Can you repeat that?

❌ I’m in the entrance.  
✅ I’m at the entrance.

今天只复习这 10 句：

> Sorry, just to clarify…

> Do you mean…?

> So, if I understand correctly…

> I see your point, but…

> I’m a bit concerned about…

> Maybe we can… instead.

> How’s your day going?

> I’m grabbing coffee. Want anything?

> Sorry, I didn’t catch that.

> How long does it usually take?

最后做一个 **90 秒自由口语挑战**：

假设今天你在新加坡公司上班：

上午会议里你没听懂一个需求，先确认；然后你觉得 deadline 太赶，委婉提出不同意见；中午同事约你买咖啡；下班后你叫 Grab，司机找不到你。

尽量把下面这些句型串起来：

> Sorry, just to clarify…  
> Do you mean…?  
> I see your point, but…  
> Maybe we can…  
> I’m grabbing coffee…  
> I’m at the pickup point…  
> I’m next to…

今天的目标不是“说得高级”，而是**听不懂敢确认、不同意会表达、生活里能快速解决问题**。
