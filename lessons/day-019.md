## Day 19｜40-minute Singapore English Speaking Lesson

今天练一组新的高频场景：**催进度但不显得强硬、需求变更时划清范围、handover/交接、会议里表达部分同意、同事临时聊天、餐厅订位与打包、Grab 改路线、取快递/门禁/服务柜台沟通**。

目标还是：**短句、自然、能直接开口。**

---

## 0–5 min｜Warm-up：催进度但不“催人”

先大声读 3 遍：

> **Just checking in — any update on this?**  
> 跟进一下，这个有更新吗？

> **Do you have an ETA for this?**  
> 这个大概什么时候能好？

> **This wasn’t part of the original scope.**  
> 这个不在原来的范围里。

> **I agree with the general direction, but I have one concern.**  
> 大方向我同意，不过我有一个顾虑。

今天先记一个很重要的职场词：

> **ETA = estimated time of arrival**

工作里不只是“到达时间”，也经常表示：

**预计什么时候完成。**

例如：

> Do you have an ETA for the API?

> What’s the ETA on the fix?

> I should have an ETA by this afternoon.

---

# 5–17 min｜Office Communication & Meetings

## 场景 1：你在等别的团队

**You:**  
Hey, just checking in — any update on the API?

**Backend Engineer:**  
We’re still working on it.

**You:**  
No problem. Do you have an ETA?

**Backend Engineer:**  
Probably by the end of the day.

**You:**  
Great. That should work for us.

这里最重要：

> **Just checking in...**

它比：

> Why is this not finished?

自然太多。

你可以套用：

> Just checking in — any update on the design?

> Just checking in — did you get a chance to look at this?

> Just checking in — are we still on track for today?

---

## 场景 2：别人临时加需求

**PM:**  
Can we also add an export button?

**You:**  
We can, but that wasn’t part of the original scope.

**PM:**  
Is it a big change?

**You:**  
Not necessarily, but I’d like to understand the requirement before we commit to it.

这句非常值得背：

> **That wasn’t part of the original scope.**

比：

> This is not my task.

专业得多。

然后继续：

> **I’d like to understand the requirement first.**

或者：

> **We should probably estimate it separately.**

= 我们最好把这部分单独评估。

---

## 场景 3：你部分同意，但有顾虑

**Colleague:**  
I think we should cache everything on the client.

**You:**  
I agree with the general direction, but I have one concern.

**Colleague:**  
What’s that?

**You:**  
We may end up with stale data.

这里不要只会：

> I agree.

> I disagree.

更自然的是：

> **I agree with the general direction, but...**

> **That makes sense, but...**

> **I see where you’re coming from, but...**

第三句：

> **I see where you’re coming from.**

= 我理解你的出发点。

不是字面“我看到你从哪里来”。

---

## 场景 4：交接任务

**Colleague:**  
I’ll be on leave tomorrow. Can you take over this issue?

**You:**  
Sure. Can you give me a quick handover before you go?

**Colleague:**  
Of course.

**You:**  
Could you also send me the latest status and anything I should watch out for?

这里：

> **give me a quick handover**

= 简单交接一下。

还有一个很实用：

> **Is there anything I should watch out for?**

= 有什么需要特别注意的吗？

适合：

- 项目交接
- 新功能上线
- 运维
- 新流程

---

# 17–24 min｜Colleague Small Talk & Socializing

## 场景 1：茶水间聊天

**Colleague:**  
Long morning?

你：

> Yeah, I’ve been in meetings since nine.

同事：

> Same here.

你：

> I need coffee before the next one.

非常自然。

这里：

> **Long morning?**

不是完整句，但口语里很正常。

你也可以听到：

> Busy day?

> Rough morning?

> Long week?

---

## 场景 2：同事问午饭

**Colleague:**  
What are you getting for lunch?

你：

> I’m not sure yet. I feel like having something light.

`feel like + doing / noun`

= 想……

例如：

> I feel like having noodles.

> I feel like getting coffee.

> I don’t feel like eating anything heavy.

注意：

❌ `I feel like to eat noodles.`

✅ **I feel like eating noodles.**

---

## 场景 3：同事说“改天一起吃饭”

**Colleague:**  
We should grab lunch sometime.

你可以说：

> Yeah, definitely. Let’s do that.

或者：

> Sure. Let me know when you’re free.

如果你想主动一点：

> How about sometime next week?

这比只说：

> Okay.

更容易把社交继续下去。

---

# 24–31 min｜Restaurant / Shopping / Ride-hailing

## 餐厅：订位

你打电话或现场问：

> **Hi, do you have a table for three around 7 p.m.?**

如果满了：

> What’s the earliest available time?

如果可以：

> 7:30 works for us.

如果想坐里面：

> Do you have any indoor seating available?

如果带小朋友：

> Do you have a high chair?

---

## 打包

你吃不完：

> **Could I get this packed to go?**

在新加坡也很自然听到：

> Can I get this takeaway?

或者更短：

> Could you pack this for me?

---

## Shopping：问能不能保留商品

> **Could you hold this for me until this evening?**

`hold` 在这里：

= 帮我保留。

例如：

> Can you hold this for 30 minutes?

> How long can you hold it for me?

---

## Grab / Taxi：临时改路线

你：

> **Could we make one quick stop on the way?**

司机：

> Where?

你：

> Just at the convenience store up ahead.

如果怕麻烦：

> Only if it’s convenient.

或者：

> No worries if it’s too much of a detour.

`detour` = 绕路。

---

# 31–36 min｜Practical Singapore Life Errands

## 场景 1：取快递 / Parcel locker

你找不到快递：

> Hi, I received a collection notification, but I can’t find the parcel.

工作人员：

> Do you have the tracking number?

你：

> Yes, here it is.

然后：

> Could you help me check where it was delivered?

高频句：

> **Could you help me check...?**

---

## 场景 2：公寓门禁

你进不了门：

> **My access card doesn’t seem to be working.**

比：

> My card is broken.

更自然，因为你还不确定是不是坏了。

继续：

> Could you help me reactivate it?

或者更简单：

> Could you check what’s wrong with it?

---

## 场景 3：服务柜台

你不知道自己要排哪个队：

> **Excuse me, am I in the right queue for this?**

或者：

> Which counter should I go to?

如果别人让你填表：

> Do I need to fill this out now?

`fill out a form`

= 填表。

---

# 36–38 min｜Common Grammar Fixes

今天纠正 5 个非常实用的。

❌ `Any update for this?`  
✅ **Any update on this?**

常用搭配：

> update on + something

---

❌ `When can finish?`  
✅ **When can it be finished?**

更自然工作英语：

✅ **Do you have an ETA?**

---

❌ `I agree your idea.`  
✅ **I agree with your idea.**

结构：

> agree with + 人/观点

---

❌ `I feel like to eat something light.`  
✅ **I feel like eating something light.**

结构：

> feel like + noun / doing

---

❌ `Can you help me to check this?`

不是严重错误，但口语更自然：

✅ **Can you help me check this?**

---

# 38–40 min｜Final Speaking Drill

先完整读：

> I’m waiting on the backend API, so I’ll check in with the team and ask for an ETA. There’s also a new request that wasn’t part of the original scope, so I think we should estimate it separately. I agree with the general direction, but I’d like to clarify one concern before we move forward.

第二遍只看关键词：

**API → check in → ETA → new request → original scope → estimate → concern → move forward**

第三遍，不看原文，自己说。

重点不是背一模一样，而是把意思说出来。

| 单独评估 | **We should estimate it separately.** |

| 理解对方 | **I see where you’re coming from.** |
| 交接 | **Can you give me a quick handover?** |
| 注意事项 | **Anything I should watch out for?** |
| 想吃某个东西 | **I feel like having...** |
| 改天约饭 | **Let me know when you’re free.** |
| 订桌 | **Do you have a table for three?** |
| 打包 | **Could you pack this for me?** |
| 临时停车 | **Could we make one quick stop?** |
| 门禁坏了 | **My access card doesn’t seem to be working.** |

今天最值得练到脱口而出的 5 句：

> **Just checking in — any update on this?**

> **Do you have an ETA?**

> **That wasn’t part of the original scope.**

> **I agree with the general direction, but I have one concern.**

> **Can you give me a quick handover?**

这组句子特别适合工程师：它们能让你在催进度、控制 scope、讨论方案和做跨团队交接时，听起来既直接又不生硬。
