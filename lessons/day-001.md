今天这节课做成 **40 分钟口语训练**，主题是：**在新加坡上班 + 日常生活时最常用的表达**。重点不是背单词，而是让你能直接开口。

## 0–10 分钟｜办公室沟通与会议

先大声读下面对话 2 遍，第二遍尽量不看中文。

**场景：会议里表达进度**

A: How’s the API integration going?  
B: It’s going well. I’ve finished the frontend part, but I’m still waiting for the backend endpoint.  
A: Do you think we can finish it by Friday?  
B: Yes, as long as there are no major changes.

中文意思：

A：API 对接怎么样了？  
B：挺顺利的。前端部分已经完成了，不过我还在等后端接口。  
A：你觉得周五前能完成吗？  
B：可以，只要没有大的改动。

今天最值得记住的 4 个句型：

- **I’ve finished ___, but I’m still waiting for ___.**
  - I’ve finished the UI, but I’m still waiting for the API.
- **Do you think we can ___ by ___?**
  - Do you think we can release it by Friday?
- **As long as ___, we should be fine.**
  - As long as there are no blockers, we should be fine.
- **I’m currently working on ___.**
  - I’m currently working on the login flow.

现在做替换训练，连续说，不要停下来翻译。

> I’ve finished the homepage, but I’m still waiting for the design.  
> I’ve finished the frontend, but I’m still waiting for the API.  
> I’ve finished the bug fix, but I’m still waiting for QA.

然后自己说 3 句，内容换成你真实工作里会遇到的任务。

### 常见错误

❌ I already finish it.  
✅ I’ve already finished it.

❌ I’m waiting the backend.  
✅ I’m waiting **for** the backend.

❌ Can we finish until Friday?  
✅ Can we finish **by Friday**?

**by Friday** = 最晚周五完成。  
**until Friday** = 某个状态一直持续到周五。

## 10–20 分钟｜和同事聊天

在新加坡办公室里，同事之间的英语通常不会特别正式。自然、简单比复杂语法更重要。

**场景：午饭前**

A: Have you had lunch yet?  
B: Not yet. I’m thinking of getting chicken rice downstairs.  
A: Sounds good. Mind if I join you?  
B: Sure. Let’s go.

重点表达：

**Have you had lunch yet?**

非常自然，相当于：

“吃午饭了吗？”

你也可能听到：

**Had lunch already?**

这是新加坡非常常见的口语式表达，但标准英语建议你自己说：

**Have you had lunch yet?**

另一个很实用的句型：

**I’m thinking of + 动名词**

比如：

> I’m thinking of getting coffee.  
> I’m thinking of working from home tomorrow.  
> I’m thinking of taking leave next Friday.

注意：

❌ I’m thinking to get coffee.  
✅ I’m thinking **of getting** coffee.

再练一个办公室社交句型：

**How was your weekend?**

回答不要只说：

> Good.

可以扩展成：

> Pretty good. I mostly stayed home and relaxed.

或者：

> It was good. I went out for dinner with some friends.

现在做 1 分钟自由输出：

假设同事问你：

**How was your weekend?**

连续回答 30–60 秒，至少包含：

- 去了哪里
- 吃了什么
- 做了什么
- 感觉怎么样

## 20–30 分钟｜餐厅、购物、打车

### 餐厅

在新加坡 Hawker Centre 或 Food Court，你会大量用到非常短的表达。

**场景：点餐**

You: Hi, can I get one chicken rice, please?  
Staff: White chicken or roasted chicken?  
You: Roasted chicken, please.  
Staff: Eat here or takeaway?  
You: Eat here.

你可能还会听到：

**Having here or takeaway?**

或者更本地化一点：

**Eat here or dabao?**

**dabao** 来自中文“打包”，在新加坡很常见。

但你自己说标准英语完全没问题：

> Takeaway, please.

几个高频句型：

**Can I get ___?**

> Can I get an iced coffee?  
> Can I get one chicken rice?  
> Can I get this without sugar?

比：

> I want…

听起来更自然。

如果你不确定辣不辣：

> Is this spicy?

如果想少辣：

> Can you make it less spicy?

如果不要香菜：

> No coriander, please.

---

### Shopping

店员问：

**Can I help you?**

你可以说：

> I’m just looking, thanks.

这是非常高频的表达。

想问有没有其他尺寸：

> Do you have this in a larger size?

或者：

> Do you have this in black?

句型：

**Do you have this in + size/color?**

---

### Grab / Taxi

司机打电话：

**Hi, where are you?**

你可以说：

> I’m at the main entrance.

或者：

> I’m outside Tower B.

如果你找不到车：

> I can’t see your car. Where are you parked?

如果想让司机稍微等一下：

> Could you wait for me for a minute?

注意：

❌ Wait me one minute.  
✅ Wait **for me** for a minute.

## 30–35 分钟｜新加坡生活办事

假设你刚到新加坡，需要处理银行、租房、手机卡等事情。

### 银行

> I’d like to open a bank account.

**I’d like to…**

是非常重要的礼貌表达。

比如：

> I’d like to apply for a credit card.  
> I’d like to change my address.  
> I’d like to make an appointment.

---

### 租房

和中介交流：

> Is this unit still available?

> How much is the monthly rent?

> Are utilities included?

> How long is the lease?

**utilities** 通常指：

水、电、燃气等费用。

另一个非常重要：

> Is the internet included?

---

### 手机卡

> I’d like to get a SIM card.

如果问流量：

> How much data does this plan include?

如果想问有没有合约：

> Is there a contract?

## 35–38 分钟｜快速纠错

把下面错误句子大声改成正确版本。

1.

❌ I’m go to Singapore for work.

✅ **I’m going to Singapore for work.**

或者：

✅ **I’m moving to Singapore for work.**

---

2.

❌ I work in a software engineer.

✅ **I work as a software engineer.**

或者：

✅ **I’m a software engineer.**

---

3.

❌ I’m waiting the API.

✅ **I’m waiting for the API.**

---

4.

❌ I want go to lunch.

✅ **I want to go to lunch.**

---

5.

❌ Yesterday I go to the office.

✅ **Yesterday I went to the office.**

---

6.

❌ I have finished it yesterday.

✅ **I finished it yesterday.**

因为 **yesterday** 是明确过去时间，所以通常用一般过去时。

对比：

> I’ve finished it.

表示“已经完成了”，强调现在结果。

## 38–40 分钟｜今日速记复习

今天只需要真正记住这 10 句：

> I’m currently working on it.

> I’ve finished the frontend, but I’m still waiting for the API.

> Do you think we can finish it by Friday?

> As long as there are no blockers, we should be fine.

> Have you had lunch yet?

> I’m thinking of getting some coffee.

> Can I get one chicken rice, please?

> I’m just looking, thanks.

> Could you wait for me for a minute?

> I’d like to open a bank account.

今天最后做一个 **60 秒口语挑战**：

假设你早上来到新加坡办公室：

你遇到同事 → 聊昨天的工作 → 参加会议 → 中午一起去吃饭。

尝试连续说：

> Morning.  
> Yesterday I finished…  
> Today I’m working on…  
> I’m still waiting for…  
> I think we can finish it by…  
> Have you had lunch yet?  
> I’m thinking of getting…

不用追求复杂句子。**连续、自然、不停顿**，比使用高级词汇更重要。
