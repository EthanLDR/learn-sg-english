今天换一组新内容，避免重复前两天。重点练你在新加坡工作里很容易遇到的：**确认优先级、争取时间、会议打断、和同事约饭、点单修改、Grab 定位、办卡/取件/预约**。

## 40-minute Singapore English Speaking Lesson

### 1) Warm-up — 5 min

先大声读 3 遍：

> **Which one should I prioritize?**  
> 我应该优先做哪一个？

> **I may need a bit more time to finish this properly.**  
> 我可能还需要一点时间才能把这个做好。

> **Can we move this to Monday?**  
> 我们可以把这个挪到周一吗？

今天先记一个很重要的职场区别：

> **finish this** = 把这件事做完  
> **finish this properly** = 把这件事真正做好

如果时间不够，不要总说：

> I cannot finish.

更自然：

> **I may need a bit more time.**

或者：

> **I don’t think I can finish this by today.**

---

# 2) Office Communication & Meetings — 12 min

## 场景 A：两个任务同时来了

**Manager:**  
Can you take a look at this bug today?

**You:**  
Sure. I’m also working on the payment feature. Which one should I prioritize?

**Manager:**  
Please fix the bug first.

**You:**  
Got it. I’ll switch to the bug first and come back to the payment feature after that.

这里最值得背：

> **Which one should I prioritize?**

比：

> Which one is more important?

更像职场英语。

再练几个：

> Which task should I prioritize?

> Should I prioritize this over the payment feature?

> Is this more urgent than the release work?

其中：

> **prioritize A over B**

= A 的优先级高于 B。

例如：

> Should we prioritize stability over new features?

---

## 场景 B：你觉得时间不现实

**Manager:**  
Can we get this done by tomorrow?

**You:**  
I’m not sure that’s realistic.

**Manager:**  
How much time do you need?

**You:**  
I think I’ll need another day to finish the implementation and testing.

**Manager:**  
So Monday?

**You:**  
Yes, Monday should be doable.

这组非常重要：

> **I’m not sure that’s realistic.**  
> 我不确定这个时间是否现实。

> **I’ll need another day.**  
> 我还需要一天。

> **Monday should be doable.**  
> 周一应该可行。

**doable** 是办公室里非常自然的词：

> Friday is doable.

> This approach is doable.

> Is that doable on your side?

最后一句尤其适合跨团队：

> **Is that doable on your side?**

= 你们那边能做到吗？

---

## 场景 C：会议里想插话

不要一直等到会议结束。

可以说：

> **Sorry, can I jump in here?**

或者：

> **Can I add something here?**

然后：

> I think there’s one risk we haven’t considered.

完整：

**You:**  
Sorry, can I jump in here?

**Colleague:**  
Sure.

**You:**  
I think there’s one risk we haven’t considered. If the API changes again, we may need to rework the frontend.

这句：

> **There’s one thing we haven’t considered.**

非常万能。

替换：

> There’s one edge case we haven’t considered.

> There’s one issue we haven’t discussed yet.

> There’s one dependency we need to confirm.

---

# 3) Colleague Small Talk & Socializing — 7 min

## 场景：同事约午饭

**Colleague:**  
We’re going for lunch. You coming?

注意，新加坡办公室口语里很容易听到这种省略表达：

> **You coming?**

完整语法是：

> Are you coming?

但日常口语中前者很常见。

你可以说：

> Yeah, give me two minutes.

或者：

> Sure. Where are you guys heading?

**Where are you guys heading?**

= 你们准备去哪？

比：

> Where are you going?

稍微更自然一点。

---

### 同事推荐食物

**Colleague:**  
Have you tried laksa yet?

**You:**  
Not yet. Is there a good place nearby?

**Colleague:**  
Yeah, there’s one downstairs.

**You:**  
Nice. I’ll give it a try.

重点：

> **Have you tried ___ yet?**

回答：

> Yeah, I’ve tried it before.

> Not yet, but I’d like to.

> No, what’s it like?

最后一句很适合聊天：

> **What’s it like?**

= 它怎么样？

例如：

> What’s the food like?

> What’s the area like?

> What’s the team culture like?

---

# 4) Restaurant / Shopping / Ride-hailing — 7 min

## Hawker / 餐厅

今天练“修改订单”。

**You:**  
Can I get the chicken rice?

**Staff:**  
Sure.

**You:**  
Could I have less rice and no skin?

**Staff:**  
Okay.

**You:**  
And could you put the sauce on the side?

三个直接背：

> **Less rice, please.**

> **No skin, please.**

> **Sauce on the side, please.**

完整句当然更礼貌：

> Could I have less rice?

但在 hawker centre，用简短表达完全正常。

---

## Shopping

你试衣服后觉得不合适：

> **It’s a bit too loose.**

> **It’s a bit too tight.**

> **Do you have one size down?**

> **Do you have one size up?**

不要说：

❌ `Do you have smaller one?`

更自然：

✅ **Do you have a smaller one?**

或者：

✅ **Do you have this in a smaller size?**

---

## Grab / Taxi

司机问：

> Which entrance are you at?

你：

> **I’m at the main entrance, near the drop-off point.**

司机找不到：

> **I’ll send you my exact location.**

如果你马上到：

> **I’ll be there in two minutes.**

注意：

**in two minutes** = 两分钟后

不是：

❌ `after two minutes`

日常英语中表达“多久之后”通常用：

> in 5 minutes

> in 10 minutes

> in half an hour

---

# 5) Practical Singapore Life Errands — 5 min

## 场景 A：取快递

> Hi, I’m here to collect a parcel.

工作人员：

> Do you have the collection code?

你：

> Yes, here it is.

如果找不到：

> **I received a notification, but I can’t find the parcel in the app.**

---

## 场景 B：预约

比如银行、诊所、理发：

> **I’d like to make an appointment for Saturday.**

别人问：

> What time works for you?

你：

> **Any time after 2 p.m. works for me.**

非常好用：

> **___ works for me.**

例如：

> Friday works for me.

> 3 p.m. works for me.

> Either option works for me.

---

## 场景 C：不知道流程

不要只说：

> What should I do?

可以说：

> **Could you walk me through the process?**

= 你可以跟我讲一下整个流程吗？

稍微简单一点：

> **What do I need to do next?**

这句在银行、政府柜台、公寓管理处都非常实用。

---

# 6) Common Grammar Fixes — 2 min

今天纠正 4 个。

❌ `Which one I should prioritize?`  
✅ **Which one should I prioritize?**

特殊疑问句：

> Wh-word + 助动词 + 主语 + 动词

---

❌ `I need one more day for finish it.`  
✅ **I need one more day to finish it.**

结构：

> need + 时间 + **to do**

---

❌ `I will arrive after 10 minutes.`  
✅ **I’ll arrive in 10 minutes.**

未来“多久之后”：

> **in + 时间**

---

❌ `This one is too much loose.`  
✅ **This one is too loose.**

不要：

> too much + adjective

直接：

> too loose  
> too expensive  
> too spicy

---

# 7) Final Speaking Drill — 2 min

现在连续说下面这段。

> I’m currently working on the payment feature, but I’ve also been asked to fix an urgent bug. I’ll check with my manager about which one I should prioritize. I don’t think I can finish both today, so I may need a bit more time. If I can get one more day, Monday should be doable.

拆成语块：

> I’m currently working on...

> I’ve also been asked to...

> Which one should I prioritize?

> I may need a bit more time.

> Monday should be doable.

然后不看原文，用这 5 个关键词自己复述：

**payment → bug → prioritize → more time → Monday**

## 今日复习卡

| 使用场景 | 直接说 |
|---|---|
| 问优先级 | **Which one should I prioritize?** |
| 时间不现实 | **I’m not sure that’s realistic.** |
| 需要更多时间 | **I may need a bit more time.** |
| 这个时间可行 | **Monday should be doable.** |
| 会议插话 | **Sorry, can I jump in here?** |
| 提醒遗漏 | **There’s one thing we haven’t considered.** |
| 同事去哪吃饭 | **Where are you guys heading?** |
| 问体验 | **What’s it like?** |
| 少一点米饭 | **Less rice, please.** |
| 酱料单独放 | **Sauce on the side, please.** |
| 这个时间可以 | **3 p.m. works for me.** |
| 下一步怎么做 | **What do I need to do next?** |

今天最建议反复练到脱口而出的 4 句是：

> **Which one should I prioritize?**  
> **I’m not sure that’s realistic.**  
> **I may need a bit more time.**  
> **Is that doable on your side?**

这四句对你做跨团队开发、评估 deadline、开项目会特别实用。
