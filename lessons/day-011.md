## Day 11｜40 分钟新加坡英语口语训练

今天的主题是：**“工作里出错后怎么说明、会议结束前怎么确认行动项、和同事自然接话，以及处理点餐、打车和日常办事。”**

今天重点不是学更多单词，而是练一些**很成熟、很自然、在新加坡办公室里能直接使用的表达**。

---

## 0–10 分钟｜办公室：发现自己出了问题，怎么自然说明

先大声读两遍。

**场景：你发现自己的代码导致了一个 bug。**

A: We’re seeing an issue with the checkout page. Do you know what happened?  
B: Yeah, I looked into it. It turns out one of my changes caused the problem.  
A: Do we know how serious it is?  
B: It only affects users on older browsers. I’ve got a fix ready, and I’m testing it now.  
A: Okay. When can we deploy it?  
B: If testing looks good, we should be able to deploy it this afternoon.

这里有 5 个非常值得记住的表达。

### 1. I looked into it.

= **我调查了一下 / 我查了一下。**

不要每次都说：

> I checked it.

工程师更自然地说：

> I looked into it.

例如：

> I looked into the issue this morning.

> I’ll look into it after this meeting.

---

### 2. It turns out…

= **结果发现……**

这是非常自然的解释句型。

> It turns out the API response changed.

> It turns out the issue is on our side.

> It turns out one of my changes caused the bug.

注意它不是非常正式，所以特别适合同事沟通。

---

### 3. One of my changes caused the problem.

这句话非常重要。

工作里如果确实是自己的改动造成的，不需要绕很久：

> One of my changes caused the problem.

然后马上接解决方案：

> I’ve already got a fix.

这种表达比一大堆解释更成熟。

---

### 4. It only affects…

> It only affects mobile users.

> It only affects users on Safari.

> It only affects new accounts.

这个模板非常适合说明影响范围。

---

### 5. If testing looks good…

= **如果测试没问题……**

> If testing looks good, we can release it today.

> If everything looks good, I’ll merge it.

### Speaking Drill

连续说三遍：

> I looked into it.

> It turns out one of my changes caused the issue.

> It only affects mobile users.

> I’ve got a fix ready.

> If testing looks good, we can deploy it this afternoon.

然后把 mobile users 换成：

> iOS users  
> new users  
> users on Safari  
> the checkout flow

## 10–17 分钟｜会议：结束前确认“谁做什么”

很多会议的问题是大家聊了很多，但结束之后：

**没人知道谁负责。**

先读对话：

A: Before we wrap up, can we quickly go over the action items?  
B: Sure. I’ll take care of the frontend changes.  
A: Great. Sarah will handle the API update.  
B: And who’s following up with QA?  
A: I can do that.  
B: Perfect. So we’re aiming to have everything ready by Thursday, right?  
A: Exactly.

今天非常重要：

### Before we wrap up…

**wrap up = 结束**

> Before we wrap up, I have one quick question.

> Before we wrap up, can we confirm the timeline?

---

### Can we go over the action items?

**action items = 会后需要执行的事项**

非常常见。

> Let’s go over the action items.

> What are the action items from this meeting?

---

### I’ll take care of…

> I’ll take care of the frontend changes.

> I’ll take care of the documentation.

---

### Who’s following up with…?

**follow up with someone**

= 和某人后续确认 / 跟进。

> Who’s following up with QA?

> I’ll follow up with the backend team.

---

### So we’re aiming to…, right?

这是会议结束时非常好的确认句。

> So we’re aiming to release on Friday, right?

> So I’ll handle the frontend and you’ll handle the API, right?

### 1 分钟 Drill

完整说：

> Before we wrap up, can we go over the action items?

> I’ll take care of the frontend.

> I’ll follow up with QA.

> So we’re aiming for Friday, right?

## 17–23 分钟｜同事闲聊：怎么加入一个正在进行的对话

场景：几个同事正在聊吃饭。

A: We were just talking about where to get lunch.  
B: Oh, have you decided yet?  
A: Not yet. Any suggestions?  
B: There’s a decent Thai place across the road.  
A: Is it good?  
B: Yeah, I’ve been there a couple of times.

这里重点是让自己自然加入聊天。

### What are you guys talking about?

同事之间可以用，但有时稍显直接。

更自然：

> What are you guys talking about?

或者：

> What did I miss?

**What did I miss?**

特别自然。

相当于：

**你们刚刚聊什么呢？**

---

### Have you decided yet?

> Have you decided where to eat yet?

> Have you decided what to order yet?

---

### Any suggestions?

= 有什么推荐？

非常万能：

> Any suggestions for lunch?

> Any suggestions for coffee around here?

---

### I’ve been there a couple of times.

不要说：

❌ I went there two times before.

更自然：

> I’ve been there a couple of times.

### 30 秒自由输出

假设同事正在讨论周末活动。

你加入：

> Hey, what did I miss?

> Oh nice.

> Have you decided where to go yet?

> Any suggestions?

## 23–28 分钟｜和同事聊“工作忙不忙”

场景：

A: Busy day?  
B: Yeah, pretty packed. I’ve got back-to-back meetings this afternoon.  
A: Same here. Hopefully things calm down tomorrow.  
B: Yeah, fingers crossed.

几个很口语的表达：

### Pretty packed.

= 安排很满。

> My afternoon is pretty packed.

> Today’s pretty packed.

---

### back-to-back meetings

= 一个接一个的会议。

> I’ve got back-to-back meetings from 2 to 5.

这个在职场非常高频。

---

### Hopefully things calm down.

= 希望接下来没那么忙。

---

### Fingers crossed.

字面是“手指交叉”，实际意思：

**希望如此 / 祈祷别出问题。**

A:

> Hopefully the release goes smoothly.

B:

> Fingers crossed.

## 28–32 分钟｜Hawker / 咖啡店：自然点单

场景：

You: Hi, one iced kopi C, less sweet, please.  
Staff: Anything else?  
You: Yeah, could I also get a kaya toast set?  
Staff: Sure. Eat here?  
You: Yes, eating here.

在新加坡你可能会听到：

**kopi C**

大致就是加淡奶的咖啡。

但重点不是背所有 Singlish，而是能听懂常见表达。

你自己依然可以使用自然标准英语：

> Less sweet, please.

> No sugar, please.

> Could I also get…?

### “再加一个”怎么说？

不要：

❌ Give me one more toast.

可以：

> Could I also get a kaya toast?

或者：

> Can I add a kaya toast?

---

### 店员问你：

> Anything else?

回答：

> That’s all, thanks.

超级高频。

---

## 32–35 分钟｜Grab：司机问你具体在哪里

场景：

Driver: Where exactly are you?  
You: I’m outside Tower 3, next to the drop-off point.  
Driver: Is that the entrance facing the main road?  
You: Yes, exactly. I’ll wait there.

重点：

### Where exactly are you?

你会经常听到。

回答模板：

> I’m outside ___.

> I’m next to ___.

> I’m opposite ___.

> I’m near ___.

例如：

> I’m outside the main lobby.

> I’m next to the taxi stand.

> I’m opposite the MRT exit.

---

### The entrance facing…

**facing = 朝向**

> the entrance facing the main road

> the entrance facing the MRT station

## 35–38 分钟｜生活办事：账户 / 地址 / 文件不齐

场景：

Staff: We need proof of your current address.  
You: I have my tenancy agreement. Would that work?  
Staff: Yes, but we also need your ID.  
You: I don’t have the physical copy with me. Would a digital copy be okay?  
Staff: Yes, that should be fine.

这里有两个非常万能的句型：

### Would that work?

= **这个可以吗？**

例如：

> I can come at 4. Would that work?

> I have a digital copy. Would that work?

---

### Would ___ be okay?

> Would a digital copy be okay?

> Would tomorrow morning be okay?

> Would PayNow be okay?

非常自然，也非常好用。

---

## 38–40 分钟｜Grammar Fix + Compact Review

今天纠正 6 个常见错误。

❌ I checked this problem already.  
✅ **I looked into the issue already.**

❌ My change make this bug.  
✅ **One of my changes caused the bug.**

❌ Before finish the meeting…  
✅ **Before we wrap up…**

❌ Who follow this task?  
✅ **Who’s following up on this?**

❌ Today I have many meetings continuously.  
✅ **I’ve got back-to-back meetings today.**

❌ Digital copy can or not?  
✅ **Would a digital copy be okay?**

这里补充一点新加坡英语：

你确实可能听到：

> Can or not?

这是很典型的 Singlish。

日常朋友之间听懂就可以。

但在工作、银行、政府机构等场合，你自己优先说：

> Would that work?

或者：

> Is that okay?

会更自然、更通用。

## 今日 Compact Review

今天只需要复习这 12 句：

> I looked into it.

> It turns out…

> One of my changes caused the issue.

> It only affects…

> If testing looks good, we can deploy it today.

> Before we wrap up…

> Can we go over the action items?

> I’ll follow up with QA.

> What did I miss?

> I’ve got back-to-back meetings.

> That’s all, thanks.

> Would a digital copy be okay?

最后做一个 **2 分钟口语输出**，尽量不要停：

上午处理 bug：

> I looked into it.  
> It turns out…  
> It only affects…  
> I’ve got a fix ready…

会议结束：

> Before we wrap up…  
> Let’s go over the action items…  
> I’ll take care of…  
> I’ll follow up with…

午饭聊天：

> What did I miss?  
> Have you decided yet?  
> Any suggestions?

晚上生活办事：

> I have a digital copy.  
> Would that work?  
> Would tomorrow morning be okay?

今天最值得练成肌肉记忆的是这四句：

**It turns out…**  
**Before we wrap up…**  
**I’ll follow up with…**  
**Would that work?**
