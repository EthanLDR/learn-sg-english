## Day 10｜40 分钟新加坡英语口语训练

今天的主题是：**“说明进度受阻、推动决策、和同事建立关系，以及处理新加坡生活里的实际问题。”**  
今天会稍微提高一点难度：不只是“会说”，而是练习**如何把一件事情解释完整**。

### 0–10 分钟｜办公室：事情被卡住，怎么解释清楚

先大声读两遍。

A: Are we still on track for the release?  
B: We’re slightly behind because we’re still waiting on the backend change.  
A: Is there anything we can do in parallel?  
B: Yes. We can finish the UI and prepare the test cases while we wait.  
A: Good. When do you need the backend change by?  
B: Ideally by tomorrow morning. Otherwise, testing may get pushed back.

今天重点练 5 个句型。

**We’re slightly behind because…**  
= 我们稍微落后了一点，因为……

> We’re slightly behind because the API isn’t ready yet.

> We’re slightly behind because we found another issue during testing.

**Is there anything we can do in parallel?**  
= 有没有什么可以并行做？

这是工程团队里非常自然的一句。

> Can we work on the UI in parallel?

**while we wait**  
= 等待期间。

> We can write the test cases while we wait.

> I’ll work on the documentation while we wait.

**Ideally by…**  
= 最好在……之前。

> Ideally by tomorrow morning.

> Ideally by the end of the day.

**Otherwise…**  
= 否则。

> Otherwise, testing may get pushed back.

> Otherwise, we might miss the deadline.

### Speaking Drill

连续说三遍：

> We’re slightly behind because we’re waiting on the backend.

> We can work on the UI in parallel.

> Ideally, we need the API by tomorrow morning.

> Otherwise, testing may get pushed back.

然后自己替换成真实项目：

> design  
> API  
> QA  
> deployment  
> security review

## 10–17 分钟｜会议：大家一直讨论，但没人做决定

场景：

A: We’ve discussed a few options. Do we need to make a decision today?  
B: I think so. Otherwise, we’ll lose another day.  
A: Which option do you recommend?  
B: I’d go with option B. It’s simpler and less risky.  
A: Are there any major downsides?  
B: Nothing major. We can always revisit it later.

重点练这些表达。

**Do we need to make a decision today?**  
= 今天是不是得做决定了？

**Which option do you recommend?**

比：

> Which one is good?

专业自然很多。

**I’d go with…**  
= 我会选择……

> I’d go with option B.

> I’d go with the simpler approach.

**It’s less risky.**

> It’s easier to maintain.

> It’s faster to implement.

**We can always revisit it later.**  
= 后面还可以再重新讨论。

这是会议里非常好用的一句话，特别适合先做一个可逆决策。

### 1 分钟表达练习

自己完整说一段：

> I’d go with option B because…  
> The main benefit is…  
> The downside is…  
> But I think it’s less risky.  
> We can always revisit it later.

## 17–23 分钟｜同事社交：新加入团队后怎么自然聊天

场景：

A: How are you settling in?  
B: Pretty well, actually. I’m still getting familiar with the codebase, but everyone’s been really helpful.  
A: Nice. Have you worked with this tech stack before?  
B: Most of it, yes. The infrastructure side is still pretty new to me.  
A: You’ll pick it up quickly.

这里很适合你以后刚到新加坡公司时用。

**How are you settling in?**  
= 适应得怎么样？

比：

> How is your new job?

更自然。

回答：

> Pretty well, actually.

> So far, so good.

> I’m still getting familiar with everything.

**I’m getting familiar with…**  
= 我正在熟悉……

> I’m getting familiar with the codebase.

> I’m getting familiar with the team’s workflow.

**___ is still pretty new to me.**  
= ……对我来说还是比较新的。

> The infrastructure side is still pretty new to me.

> The product domain is still pretty new to me.

这个句型比：

> I don’t know infrastructure.

自然很多。

## 23–28 分钟｜同事闲聊：邀请别人一起吃饭

你不一定每次等别人邀请你。

场景：

You: Have you had lunch yet?  
Colleague: Not yet.  
You: I’m heading downstairs in a bit. Want to grab something together?  
Colleague: Sure. Where are you thinking?  
You: Maybe the hawker centre across the road.  
Colleague: Sounds good.

重点：

**I’m heading downstairs in a bit.**  
= 我过一会儿要下楼。

**in a bit** = 过一会儿。

> I’ll join you in a bit.

**Want to grab something together?**  
= 要不要一起吃点东西？

**Where are you thinking?**  
= 你想去哪？

在口语里非常自然。

### 30 秒 Drill

> Have you had lunch yet?

> I’m heading downstairs in a bit.

> Want to grab something together?

> Maybe the hawker centre across the road.

## 28–32 分钟｜Hawker Centre：打包、少饭、加蛋

场景：

You: Hi, one chicken rice, please. Takeaway.  
Staff: Chilli?  
You: Yes, but just a little. Could I have less rice and add an egg?  
Staff: Sure.  
You: Thanks. How much is it altogether?

新加坡你会经常听到：

> Having here or takeaway?

或者更本地：

> Eat here or dabao?

你自己可以继续说标准英语：

> Takeaway, please.

重点句型：

**Could I have less rice?**

**Could I add an egg?**

**How much is it altogether?**  
= 一共多少钱？

**altogether** 在结账时很常用。

还有：

> Can I pay by PayNow?

> Do you take card?

这里的 **take card** = 接受刷卡。

## 32–35 分钟｜Grab：司机走错入口

场景：

Driver: I’m here.  
You: I don’t think I can see you. Which entrance are you at?  
Driver: The one next to the mall.  
You: Ah, I’m at the office entrance on the other side.  
Driver: Okay, I’ll come around.  
You: Thanks. I’ll wait by the lobby.

重点：

**I don’t think I can see you.**

比：

> I cannot find you.

更自然。

**Which entrance are you at?**

**I’m on the other side.**  
= 我在另一边。

**I’ll wait by the lobby.**

这里：

> wait by = 在……旁边等。

## 35–38 分钟｜生活办事：问银行 / 电信客服一个问题

场景：

You: Hi, I have a question about my account.  
Staff: Sure. What can I help you with?  
You: I noticed a charge that I don’t recognize. Could you help me check what it’s for?  
Staff: Sure. Let me pull up your account.  
You: Thanks.

重点表达：

**I noticed…**  
= 我注意到……

> I noticed an extra charge.

> I noticed my bill is higher this month.

**I don’t recognize this charge.**  
= 这笔扣款我不认识。

**Could you help me check what it’s for?**

非常实用。

你也可以用在电话套餐：

> Could you help me check why my bill is higher this month?

## 38–40 分钟｜Grammar Fix + Compact Review

今天纠正 6 个常见错误。

❌ We are delay because backend.  
✅ **We’re slightly behind because we’re waiting on the backend.**

❌ Can we parallel do this?  
✅ **Can we do this in parallel?**

❌ Which option you recommend?  
✅ **Which option do you recommend?**

❌ This thing is new for me.  
✅ **This is still pretty new to me.**

❌ I cannot see where are you.  
✅ **I can’t see you. Which entrance are you at?**

❌ What is this money for?  
✅ **Could you help me check what this charge is for?**

### 今日 Compact Review

今天优先复习这 12 句：

> We’re slightly behind because…

> Can we do this in parallel?

> Ideally, we need it by tomorrow morning.

> Otherwise, testing may get pushed back.

> Which option do you recommend?

> I’d go with option B.

> We can always revisit it later.

> How are you settling in?

> This is still pretty new to me.

> Want to grab something together?

> Which entrance are you at?

> Could you help me check what this charge is for?

最后做一个 **2 分钟自由输出**。不要停下来翻译，直接说：

上午项目会议：

> We’re slightly behind because…  
> We can do… in parallel.  
> Ideally…  
> Otherwise…

方案讨论：

> I’d go with…  
> The main benefit is…  
> The downside is…  
> We can always revisit it later.

午饭：

> Have you had lunch yet?  
> Want to grab something together?

晚上打车和处理账单：

> Which entrance are you at?  
> I’m on the other side.
