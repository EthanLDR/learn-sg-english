# Day 25 | Making clear requests and following up without sounding pushy

### Theme: 把请求说得具体、好执行；需要催进度时，既明确又不显得咄咄逼人

今天重点练一个全栈工程师每天都会用到的能力：**向同事请求 review、确认、数据或支持时，把背景、动作和时间点说清楚；没有回复时，礼貌跟进并说明为什么需要它。**

你会反复练这几个动作：

- 给最少但必要的背景：**A quick bit of context...**
- 明确请求动作：**Could you take a look at this when you get a chance?**
- 说清时间点：**Could you confirm this by 3 p.m.?**
- 礼貌跟进：**Just following up on this in case it got buried.**
- 解释为什么现在需要：**If I get your feedback by noon, I should be able to finish by Friday.**

同时继续复习：**I’m still waiting... / should be able to / by / on / before / future if**。

---

## 0–5 min | Warm-up：把“请求 + 时间 + 原因”说顺

下面 5 句，每句大声说 3 遍。

> **Could you take a look at this when you get a chance?**  
> 你有空的时候能帮我看一下吗？

> **Could you confirm this by 3 p.m.?**  
> 你能在下午 3 点前确认一下吗？

> **Just following up on this in case it got buried.**  
> 跟进一下，怕这条消息被其他消息淹没了。

> **I’m still waiting for the API review.**  
> 我还在等 API review。

> **If I get your feedback by noon, I should be able to finish the change by Friday.**  
> 如果中午前拿到你的反馈，我应该能在周五前完成修改。

快速复习：

- **by 3 p.m.** = 最迟下午 3 点
- **on Friday** = 周五当天
- **before Friday** = 周五之前，不包括周五

Future `if`：

> **If you confirm it today, I can update the implementation tomorrow.**

不是：

> ❌ If you will confirm it today...

# 5–17 min | Office Communication & Meetings

## 5–9 min | 场景 1：请同事 review PR，但不要只丢一个链接

**You:**  
Hey, when you get a chance, could you take a look at this PR?

**Teammate:**  
Sure. Anything specific I should focus on?

**You:**  
Yes. A quick bit of context: I changed the retry logic for failed requests. Could you mainly check the error-handling path and whether the state reset looks safe?

**Teammate:**  
Got it. When do you need the review by?

**You:**  
If possible, by 3 p.m. I’m still waiting for this review before I merge, and I’d like to start testing this afternoon.

这里最值得练的是一个三段式请求：

> **Context → Ask → Timing**

例如：

> A quick bit of context: this changes the login retry flow.  
> Could you check the error-handling path?  
> If possible, could you have a look by 3 p.m.?

比只发：

> Please review this PR.

更容易让别人快速行动。

### Reusable Pattern

> **A quick bit of context: ___. Could you ___? If possible, by ___.**

替换：

> A quick bit of context: product changed the empty-state copy. Could you confirm the final wording? If possible, by noon.

> A quick bit of context: this endpoint is now blocking frontend integration. Could you confirm the response shape? If possible, before the afternoon sync.

---

## 9–13 min | 场景 2：对方没回复，怎么催而不显得攻击性很强

你昨天已经问过一次，但没有回复。

**You:**  
Hey, just following up on this in case it got buried.

**Teammate:**  
Sorry, I missed it. What do you need from me?

**You:**  
No worries. I just need confirmation on whether `status` can be null. I’m still waiting on that before I finalize the frontend handling.

**Teammate:**  
I’ll check now.

**You:**  
Thanks. If you can confirm it by noon, I should be able to finish the integration today.

很好用的跟进句：

> **Just following up on this.**

> **Just bumping this in case you missed it.**

> **When you get a chance, could you confirm ___?**

> **I’m checking because ___.**

`bump` 在聊天工具里很口语，意思是把消息重新顶一下；正式邮件里用 `follow up` 更稳妥。

避免一上来就写：

> Why haven’t you replied?

不是语法问题，而是协作语气通常太强。

---

## 13–15 min | 场景 3：请求很急，但不假装“ASAP”什么都能解决

**Manager:**  
Can we get the final estimate today?

**You:**  
I can give you a rough estimate now, but I’m still waiting for the backend team to confirm one dependency.

**Manager:**  
When can you give me a reliable number?

**You:**  
If they confirm it before 2 p.m., I should be able to give you a more reliable estimate by 3 p.m.

**Manager:**  
Okay. Can you follow up with them now?

**You:**  
Sure. I’ll message them with the specific question and the time we need the answer by.

重点：真正好执行的“急”通常包含两个东西：

> **具体要什么** + **最晚什么时候要**

比如：

> Could you confirm whether pagination is required **by 2 p.m.**?

比：

> Please confirm ASAP.

更清楚。

---

## 15–17 min | 2-minute Oral Substitution Drill

不要默读，直接快速替换。

### Pattern 1

> **Could you take a look at the error-handling path?**

替换：

- the API contract
- the loading state
- the test failure
- the analytics event

### Pattern 2

> **Just following up on this in case it got buried.**

替换：

- Just following up on the design review.
- Just checking whether you had a chance to look at this.
- Just bumping this in case you missed it.
- When you get a chance, could you confirm the final copy?

### Pattern 3

> **If I get your feedback by noon, I should be able to finish by Friday.**

替换：

- confirmation by 2 p.m. / update the estimate by 3 p.m.
- the API response today / test tomorrow
- approval before lunch / merge this afternoon
- the design by Wednesday / release on Friday

# 17–23 min | Colleague Small Talk & Socializing

## 17–20 min | 场景 1：想请同事顺手帮个忙

**You:**  
Hey, are you heading downstairs for coffee later?

**Colleague:**  
Yeah, probably around 3.

**You:**  
Could you give me a shout when you go? I might join you.

**Colleague:**  
Sure.

**You:**  
Thanks. I’m still waiting for one meeting to finish, but if it ends by 3, I should be free.

几个自然表达：

> **Could you give me a shout when you go?**  
> 你走的时候叫我一下。

> **Let me know when you’re heading out.**  
> 你准备走的时候告诉我一下。

> **I might join you.**  
> 我可能一起去。

这里的请求很轻，不需要每次都说得很正式。

---

## 20–23 min | 场景 2：别人邀请你，但你现在不能马上答应

**Colleague:**  
We’re having dinner after work. Are you in?

**You:**  
I’d like to. Can I confirm with you by 5?

**Colleague:**  
Sure.

**You:**  
I’m still waiting to see whether I need to stay for a late call. If the call is cancelled, I should be able to join.

很好用：

> **Can I confirm with you by 5?**

这里 `confirm with you` 很自然，因为你是在说“我最迟 5 点给你确认答复”。

再注意：

> **If the call is cancelled, I should be able to join.**

不是：

> ❌ If the call will be cancelled...

# 23–30 min | Restaurant / Shopping / Ride-hailing

## 23–25 min | Restaurant：有具体需求时，直接说清楚

**You:**  
Could I get the sauce on the side, please?

**Staff:**  
Sure.

**You:**  
And could you make it less spicy if possible?

**Staff:**  
Yes, we can do mild.

**You:**  
Perfect, mild is fine. Thanks.

几个高频请求结构：

> **Could I get ___, please?**

> **Could you make it ___ if possible?**

> **Could I have ___ on the side?**

比如：

> Could I have the dressing on the side?

> Could you make it less sweet if possible?

---

## 25–27 min | Shopping：请店员帮你留货到某个时间

**You:**  
I’m still deciding between these two. Would it be possible to hold this one for me until this evening?

**Staff:**  
We can hold it until 7 p.m.

**You:**  
That works. If I decide to take it, I’ll come back before 7.

**Staff:**  
No problem.

这里继续复习：

> **until 7 p.m.** = 一直保留到 7 点

> **before 7 p.m.** = 7 点之前回来

> **by 7 p.m.** = 最迟 7 点完成某件事

Future `if`：

> **If I decide to take it, I’ll come back.**

不是：

> ❌ If I will decide...

---

## 27–30 min | Grab：给司机一个明确、可执行的位置请求

**Driver:**  
I’m approaching the building.

**You:**  
Could you pick me up at Pickup Point B instead of the main entrance?

**Driver:**  
Sure. Are you there already?

**You:**  
Not yet. I’m still waiting for the lift. I should be there in about two minutes.

**Driver:**  
Okay, I’ll wait there.

**You:**  
Thanks. I’ll message you once I’m downstairs.

这里的结构很实用：

> **Could you ___ at ___ instead of ___?**

> **I should be there in about ___ minutes.**

> **I’ll message you once I’m ___.**

`once` 表示“等……一发生就……”，例如：

> I’ll let you know once I’m ready.

# 30–35 min | Practical Singapore Life Errands

## 30–33 min | 场景 1：维修已经报过，今天需要一个明确时间

**You:**  
Hi, I’m following up on a maintenance request. I’m still waiting for someone to check the air conditioner.

**Staff:**  
We have your request here.

**You:**  
Thanks. Could you let me know whether someone can come by tomorrow afternoon?

**Staff:**  
We should be able to send someone between 2 and 4 p.m.

**You:**  
Great. If the timing changes, could you let me know before noon tomorrow?

这里的重点是把请求变成对方可以执行的动作：

> **Could you let me know whether ___?**

> **Could you let me know before ___ if anything changes?**

比单纯说：

> Please update me.

信息更完整。

---

## 33–35 min | 场景 2：办事材料还差一个确认

**You:**  
Hi, I just want to confirm one document requirement before I come down on Friday.

**Staff:**  
Sure. What would you like to check?

**You:**  
Do I need to bring the original proof of address, or is a digital copy enough?

**Staff:**  
Please bring the original.

**You:**  
Got it. I’m still waiting for the appointment reference number. If I don’t receive it by Thursday afternoon, should I call again?

继续复习：

- **on Friday** = 周五当天去
- **by Thursday afternoon** = 最迟周四下午收到
- **before I come down** = 我过去之前

# 35–38 min | Common Grammar Fixes

今天只纠正确实有问题的句子。

### 1

❌ **Can you help to review this PR me?**  
✅ **Can you help me review this PR?**  
✅ **Can you help me to review this PR?**

`help someone do` 和 `help someone to do` 都可以。

---

### 2

❌ **I’m still waiting your feedback.**  
✅ **I’m still waiting for your feedback.**

`wait for + 人 / 事物`。

---

### 3

❌ **Could you confirm me this by noon?**  
✅ **Could you confirm this by noon?**  
✅ **Could you confirm this with me by noon?**

`confirm` 不直接用 `confirm me something`。

---

### 4

❌ **If you will confirm it today, I can merge tomorrow.**  
✅ **If you confirm it today, I can merge tomorrow.**

Future `if` 条件从句通常用一般现在时。

---

### 5

❌ **I should can finish it by Friday.**  
✅ **I should be able to finish it by Friday.**

注意下面三句都正确，只是意思不同：

> **Please send it by Friday.** = 最迟周五发

> **Please send it on Friday.** = 周五当天发

> **Please send it before Friday.** = 周五之前发

---

# 38–40 min | Final Speaking Challenge

## 第 1 分钟：Workplace Challenge

只看关键词，自己说 45–60 秒：

**context → PR review → specific area → waiting → by 3 p.m. → testing → follow-up**

参考版本：

> Hey, when you get a chance, could you take a look at this PR? A quick bit of context: I changed the retry logic for failed requests. Could you mainly check the error-handling path and the state reset? I’m still waiting for the review before I merge. If you can have a look by 3 p.m., I should be able to start testing this afternoon. If you’re busy, no problem—just let me know and I’ll find another reviewer.

## 第 2 分钟：Everyday Challenge

关键词：

**maintenance → still waiting → tomorrow afternoon → 2 to 4 → changes → before noon**

参考版本：

> Hi, I’m following up on a maintenance request. I’m still waiting for someone to check the air conditioner. Could you let me know whether someone can come tomorrow afternoon? If the visit is scheduled between 2 and 4 p.m., that works for me. If the timing changes, could you let me know before noon tomorrow?

# Day 25 Compact Review

| 场景 | 直接说 |
|---|---|
| 给背景 | **A quick bit of context...** |
| 请求 review | **Could you take a look at this when you get a chance?** |
| 指定检查重点 | **Could you mainly check ___?** |
| 明确截止时间 | **Could you confirm this by 3 p.m.?** |
| 礼貌跟进 | **Just following up on this.** |
| 防止消息被淹没 | **Just following up in case it got buried.** |
| 还在等待 | **I’m still waiting for...** |
| 解释为什么要催 | **I’m checking because ___.** |
| 条件 + 时间 | **If I get your feedback by noon, I should be able to finish by Friday.** |
| 最迟某时 | **by 3 p.m. / by Friday** |
| 某天当天 | **on Friday** |
| 某时间之前 | **before noon / before Friday** |
| 轻量请求 | **Let me know when you’re heading out.** |
| 服务请求 | **Could you let me know whether ___?** |

今天最值得练到脱口而出的 6 句：

> **A quick bit of context...**

> **Could you take a look at this when you get a chance?**

> **Just following up on this in case it got buried.**

> **I’m still waiting for the API review.**

> **Could you confirm this by 3 p.m.?**

> **If I get your feedback by noon, I should be able to finish by Friday.**
