## Day 20｜40-minute Singapore English Speaking Lesson

今天练一组更接近真实工作流的表达：**需求冲突时怎么 push back、如何讲 trade-off、什么时候需要 escalate、怎么发简短 async update、同事间自然闲聊、hawker 点餐、Grab 上车、MRT/物业/柜台办事**。

重点依旧是：**少背单词，多练能直接说出口的句型。**

---

## 0–5 min｜Warm-up：表达风险和取舍

先大声读 3 遍：

> **There’s a trade-off here.**  
> 这里有一个取舍。

> **If we want this by Friday, we’ll probably need to cut the scope.**  
> 如果我们想周五完成，可能需要缩小范围。

> **I don’t think we should rush this.**  
> 我觉得这个不应该赶。

> **I’ll send a quick update once I have more information.**  
> 我有更多信息后会发一个简短更新。

今天先记一个很重要的词：

> **trade-off**

意思不是单纯“缺点”，而是：

**为了得到 A，需要牺牲 B。**

例如：

> There’s a trade-off between speed and quality.

> There’s a trade-off between simplicity and flexibility.

> We need to think about the trade-offs.

# 5–17 min｜Office Communication & Meetings

## 场景 1：时间不够，要缩范围

**Manager:**  
Can we still release everything on Friday?

**You:**  
I think Friday is possible, but we’ll probably need to cut the scope.

**Manager:**  
What would you leave out?

**You:**  
I’d keep the core flow and move the export feature to the next release.

这里最值得记：

> **If we want this by Friday, we’ll need to cut the scope.**

以及：

> **I’d keep A and move B to the next release.**

替换：

> I’d keep the core functionality and move the nice-to-have features later.

> I’d keep the current UI and postpone the redesign.

---

## 场景 2：你觉得方案太冒险

**Colleague:**  
Why don’t we just rewrite this part now?

**You:**  
I’m not against it, but I think it’s a bit risky this close to the release.

**Colleague:**  
What’s the main risk?

**You:**  
We may introduce new bugs and not have enough time to test properly.

重点句型：

> **I’m not against it, but...**

意思是：

“我不是反对，但……”

比：

> I disagree.

柔和很多。

再练：

> I’m not against the idea, but I’d prefer to do it after the release.

> I’m not against changing it, but we need more testing.

---

## 场景 3：什么时候需要 escalate

**You:**  
We’ve been waiting on approval for two days.

**Colleague:**  
Should we escalate it?

**You:**  
Probably. It’s starting to affect the timeline.

`escalate` 在工作里表示：

**把问题升级给更高层或更合适的人处理。**

你可以说：

> **I think we should escalate this.**

> **This is starting to block the team.**

> **It’s beginning to affect the timeline.**

不要把 escalate 理解成“吵大”。

它通常是：

**正常的项目管理动作。**

---

## 场景 4：发 async update

你不一定每件事都要开会。

可以说：

> **I’ll send an async update later today.**

或者更自然：

> I’ll drop a quick update in the group chat.

内容可以这样说：

> Quick update: the frontend work is done. I’m still waiting on the API change. Once that’s ready, I should be able to finish testing today.

这个结构非常好用：

> **Quick update: ...**

> **I’m still waiting on...**

> **Once that’s ready, I should be able to...**

# 17–24 min｜Colleague Small Talk & Socializing

## 场景 1：同事问你忙不忙

**Colleague:**  
Busy today?

你：

> A little. I’ve got a couple of things to finish before lunch.

或者：

> Not too bad. Just trying to clear a few things before the afternoon meetings.

`clear a few things`

= 把一些事情处理掉。

非常自然。

---

## 场景 2：午饭问“吃什么”

**Colleague:**  
What do you feel like eating?

你：

> Maybe something local. I haven’t had laksa in a while.

或者：

> I’m okay with anything, as long as it’s not too heavy.

注意：

> **as long as...**

= 只要……

例如：

> I’m fine with anywhere as long as it’s nearby.

> I’m okay with anything as long as it’s not too spicy.

---

## 场景 3：主动加入同事

你看到同事准备出去：

> Are you guys heading out for lunch?

同事：

> Yeah.

你：

> Mind if I join?

`Mind if I join?`

= 介意我一起吗？

非常自然。

也可以更直接：

> Can I join you guys?

# 24–31 min｜Restaurant / Shopping / Ride-hailing

## Hawker centre：点餐

今天练新加坡很实用的一组。

你：

> One fish soup, please.

如果不要牛奶：

> **No milk, please.**

少饭：

> **Less rice, please.**

不要辣：

> **No chilli, please.**

如果想外带：

> **Takeaway, please.**

在 hawker centre，短句非常正常，不需要每一句都完整。

---

## 饮料：理解新加坡口语环境

如果你在咖啡店听到：

> Hot or iced?

> Less sweet?

> Takeaway?

你可以简单回答：

> Iced, please.

> Less sweet, please.

> Having here.

或者标准一些：

> For here, please.

---

## Shopping：比较价格

> **Is this the final price?**

> **Is there any discount if I buy two?**

> **Is this promotion still valid?**

`valid`

= 有效。

例如：

> Is this voucher still valid?

---

## Grab / Taxi：上车点找不到

司机：

> Which pickup point are you at?

你：

> I’m at Pickup Point B, near the main entrance.

如果司机错过：

> I think you just passed the entrance.

如果你改位置：

> I’ll walk to the other pickup point. It might be easier.

这里很好用：

> **It might be easier if...**

例如：

> It might be easier if I come to you.

---

# 31–36 min｜Practical Singapore Life Errands

## 场景 1：MRT / 卡片问题

你发现卡刷不过：

> **My card isn’t working at the gate.**

然后：

> Could you help me check it?

如果余额问题：

> Do I need to top it up?

如果想知道余额：

> How can I check the balance?

---

## 场景 2：物业 / Condo 管理处

> Hi, I’d like to register a visitor.

或者：

> I’m expecting a delivery later today.

如果要问停车：

> Is visitor parking available?

如果不知道流程：

> Do I need to register the car in advance?

---

## 场景 3：柜台确认是否完成

你办完事情后：

> **Is there anything else I need to do?**

> **Is everything done on my side?**

> **Will I receive a confirmation message?**

这三句银行、政府柜台、物业都非常实用。

---

# 36–38 min｜Common Grammar Fixes

今天纠正 5 个高频错误。

❌ `We need cut the scope.`  
✅ **We need to cut the scope.**

---

❌ `This will affect to the timeline.`  
✅ **This will affect the timeline.**

`affect` 后面直接接宾语。

---

❌ `I’m not against to change it.`  
✅ **I’m not against changing it.**

结构：

> against + noun / doing

---

❌ `I will send update later.`  
✅ **I’ll send an update later.**

`update` 是可数名词时需要：

> an update

---

❌ `Can I join with you?`  
✅ **Can I join you?**

或者：

✅ **Can I join you guys?**

不需要 `with`。

---

# 38–40 min｜Final Speaking Drill

先完整读：

> We can probably still release on Friday, but there’s a trade-off. If we want to keep the deadline, we’ll need to cut the scope. I’d keep the core flow and move the lower-priority features to the next release. I’m also waiting on one approval, and if that starts affecting the timeline, I think we should escalate it.

第二遍只看关键词：

**Friday → trade-off → cut scope → core flow → next release → approval → timeline → escalate**

第三遍自己说，不要逐字翻译。

# Day 20 Compact Review

| 场景 | 最自然表达 |
|---|---|
| 讲取舍 | **There’s a trade-off here.** |
| 缩范围 | **We’ll need to cut the scope.** |
| 不完全反对 | **I’m not against it, but...** |
| 风险太大 | **I think it’s a bit risky.** |
| 升级问题 | **I think we should escalate this.** |
| 影响项目 | **It’s starting to affect the timeline.** |
| 异步更新 | **I’ll send a quick update later.** |
| 什么都行 | **I’m okay with anything.** |
| 只要…… | **As long as...** |
| 可以一起吗 | **Mind if I join?** |
| MRT 卡不能用 | **My card isn’t working at the gate.** |
| 我这边办完了吗 | **Is everything done on my side?** |

今天最值得练到脱口而出的 5 句：

> **There’s a trade-off here.**

> **If we want this by Friday, we’ll need to cut the scope.**

> **I’m not against it, but I think it’s a bit risky.**

> **I think we should escalate this.**

> **Mind if I join?**

这组句子会让你在会议里不只是“汇报进度”，而是更自然地参与**scope、风险、deadline 和决策讨论**。
