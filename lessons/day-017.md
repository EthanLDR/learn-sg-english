## Day 17｜40-minute Singapore English Speaking Lesson

今天重点练你在新加坡工作里很容易遇到、但前面没有系统覆盖的一组场景：**会议里给建议、礼貌打断、确认 deadline、向同事求助、午饭闲聊、餐厅改单、购物退换、Grab 沟通，以及物业/银行等生活办事**。目标还是一样：少背复杂语法，多练能直接说出口的句型。

### 0–5 min｜Warm-up：让表达更自然

先连续读 3 遍：

> **From my perspective, the simpler option is probably better.**  
> 从我的角度看，更简单的方案可能更好。

> **Can I quickly clarify one thing?**  
> 我可以快速确认一件事吗？

> **Are we still on track for Friday?**  
> 我们现在还是能按计划周五完成吗？

> **I could use a second opinion on this.**  
> 这个我想听听别人的意见。

今天特别值得记的是：

> **Are we still on track for Friday?**

`on track` 在工作里非常高频，意思是：

**还在按计划推进，没有偏离进度。**

例如：

> We’re on track for the release.

> Are we still on track for tomorrow?

> We’re slightly behind, but we should still be on track for Friday.

## 5–17 min｜Office Communication & Meetings

### 场景 1：会议里提出建议

**PM:**  
Should we add another confirmation step?

**You:**  
From my perspective, the simpler flow is probably better.

**PM:**  
Why?

**You:**  
Because adding another step may create more friction for users.

**PM:**  
So what would you suggest?

**You:**  
I’d keep the current flow and improve the error message instead.

这里重点练：

> **From my perspective...**  
> 从我的角度来看……

> **What I’d suggest is...**  
> 我的建议是……

> **I’d keep...**  
> 我会保留……

替换训练：

> From my perspective, this is more of a backend issue.

> From my perspective, we don’t need to solve this right now.

> What I’d suggest is shipping the smaller version first.

最后一句比：

> I suggest we ship...

更柔和。

---

### 场景 2：礼貌打断别人

会议里别人讲得很久，你想补充：

> **Sorry to interrupt, but can I quickly add something?**

更简短：

> **Can I jump in for a second?**

然后：

> I think we may be missing one edge case.

或者：

> I just want to clarify one thing before we move on.

完整练习：

**Colleague:**  
So we’ll update the API and release it tomorrow...

**You:**  
Sorry to interrupt, but can I quickly clarify one thing?

**Colleague:**  
Sure.

**You:**  
Do we need to retest the mobile flow after the API change?

这是非常自然的会议英语。

---

### 场景 3：确认进度和 deadline

**Manager:**  
How are we looking for Friday?

你不要只说：

> Good.

可以说：

> **We’re mostly on track.**

如果有一点风险：

> We’re mostly on track, but testing may take a bit longer than expected.

如果比较危险：

> There’s a chance we may slip by a day.

`slip` 在项目语境里经常表示：

**延期一点。**

例如：

> The release may slip to Monday.

但如果你觉得这个词暂时不熟，简单说：

> We may need one more day.

也完全自然。

---

### 场景 4：向同事求助

不要只说：

> Can you help me?

可以更具体：

> **Could you take a quick look at this?**

> **I could use a second opinion on this.**

完整对话：

**You:**  
Hey, do you have a minute?

**Colleague:**  
Sure.

**You:**  
I could use a second opinion on this API design.

**Colleague:**  
What’s the issue?

**You:**  
I’m not sure whether we should keep the current response format or simplify it.

非常实用的开场：

> **Do you have a minute?**

比：

> Are you free?

更适合工作里的短沟通。

---

## 17–24 min｜Colleague Small Talk & Socializing

### 场景 1：午饭前

**Colleague:**  
Have you decided what you’re having for lunch?

**You:**  
Not yet. I’m trying not to eat anything too heavy today.

**Colleague:**  
Want to get chicken rice?

**You:**  
Sure, as long as it’s not too crowded.

这里：

> **anything too heavy**

= 太油、太撑、太重口的一餐。

例如：

> I don’t want anything too heavy.

> I had a pretty heavy lunch yesterday.

---

### 场景 2：同事问工作适应得怎么样

**Colleague:**  
How are you settling into the team?

你可以说：

> Pretty well. I’m still learning how everything works, but everyone’s been really helpful.

或者：

> Pretty well. I’m getting more comfortable with the meetings now.

这里：

> **settle into...**

= 慢慢适应。

例如：

> I’m still settling into the new role.

> It took me a while to settle into the team.

---

### 场景 3：下班闲聊

**Colleague:**  
Any plans after work?

你：

> I’ll probably hit the gym and then grab dinner.

或者：

> Nothing special. I’ll probably just head home.

`probably` 是特别好用的口语词。

不要总说：

> I will go to the gym.

日常聊天更自然：

> **I’ll probably go to the gym.**

---

## 24–31 min｜Restaurant / Shopping / Ride-hailing

### 餐厅：发现点错了

你可以说：

> **Sorry, I think I ordered the wrong one.**

然后：

> Could I change it to the grilled chicken instead?

如果菜还没做：

> Is it still possible to change the order?

如果已经做好了，就不要强求，问：

> No worries if it’s too late.

这句很自然：

> **No worries if it’s too late.**

---

### 餐厅：催菜但保持礼貌

> **Hi, just checking — is my order coming soon?**

或者：

> Do you know roughly how much longer it’ll take?

注意：

❌ `How long still need?`

✅ **How much longer will it take?**

或者更口语：

> **How much longer?**

---

### Shopping：退换商品

> Hi, I bought this yesterday, but it doesn’t fit.

> Could I exchange it for a larger size?

如果想退：

> Is it possible to get a refund?

店员问：

> Do you have the receipt?

你：

> Yes, I have it here.

三个词记住：

> exchange = 换货

> refund = 退款

> receipt = 小票

---

### Grab / Taxi

司机发：

> I’m here.

但你没看到：

> I’m at the pickup point, but I can’t see your car.

如果需要司机往前：

> Could you move forward a little?

如果你走过去：

> I’ll walk over to you.

如果你马上到：

> I’ll be there in about two minutes.

---

## 31–36 min｜Practical Singapore Life Errands

### 场景 1：物业报修

> Hi, I’d like to report a problem with the water heater.

工作人员：

> What’s wrong with it?

你：

> It turns on, but the water isn’t getting hot.

然后：

> Could someone come and check it today?

如果不是很急：

> It’s not urgent, but I’d like to get it fixed sometime this week.

这里：

> **It’s not urgent, but...**

非常实用，也非常礼貌。

---

### 场景 2：银行 / 柜台确认费用

> Are there any fees for this?

> Is there a monthly fee?

> Is there anything I need to pay upfront?

`upfront`：

= 一开始就要支付的。

例如：

> Is there an upfront fee?

---

### 场景 3：办事时没听懂

如果工作人员说太快：

> Sorry, could you go over that again?

如果还是不懂：

> Could you explain that in a simpler way?

如果只想确认某一步：

> So I need to submit this form first, right?

这个：

> **..., right?**

在生活沟通里非常高频。

---

## 36–38 min｜Common Grammar Fixes

今天纠正 5 个高频错误。

❌ `Are we still can finish by Friday?`  
✅ **Can we still finish by Friday?**

或者更自然：

✅ **Are we still on track for Friday?**

---

❌ `I suggest to change this.`  
✅ **I suggest changing this.**

或者：

✅ **I suggest that we change this.**

`suggest` 后面不要直接：

> suggest to do

---

❌ `I need your advise.`  
✅ **I need your advice.**

注意：

> advice = 名词

> advise = 动词

例如：

> Can you advise me on this?

---

❌ `I will probably to go home.`  
✅ **I’ll probably go home.**

`probably` 后面直接跟动词：

> I’ll probably eat out.

> I’ll probably stay home.

---

❌ `Can I change another one?`  
如果你想说“可以换成另一个吗”：

✅ **Can I change it to another one?**

如果是换货：

✅ **Can I exchange it for another one?**

---

## 38–40 min｜Final Speaking Drill

先完整读：

> We’re mostly on track for Friday, but I’d like to double-check one dependency before we commit to the release. From my perspective, the simpler option is safer. I could use a second opinion on the API design, so I’ll ask one of the backend engineers to take a quick look.

第二遍只看关键词：

**Friday → dependency → simpler → safer → second opinion → backend engineer**

第三遍自己说。

不要追求一模一样，只要意思完整、说得自然就可以。

## Day 17 Compact Review

| 场景 | 最自然表达 |
|---|---|
| 进度正常 | **We’re on track.** |
| 确认周五进度 | **Are we still on track for Friday?** |
| 给出观点 | **From my perspective...** |
| 提建议 | **What I’d suggest is...** |
| 礼貌打断 | **Can I jump in for a second?** |
| 找同事帮看 | **Could you take a quick look at this?** |
| 听第二意见 | **I could use a second opinion.** |
| 适应团队 | **I’m still settling into the team.** |
| 改订单 | **Could I change it to...?** |
| 还要多久 | **How much longer will it take?** |
| 换货 | **Could I exchange it for a larger size?** |
| 报修不紧急 | **It’s not urgent, but...** |

今天最值得反复说的 4 句：

> **Are we still on track for Friday?**

> **From my perspective, the simpler option is probably better.**

> **Can I jump in for a second?**

> **I could use a second opinion on this.**

把这四句练熟以后，你在会议里会更容易从“回答问题”升级到**主动参与讨论、给建议、确认风险和寻求反馈**。
